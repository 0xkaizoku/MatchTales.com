## Why this race matters

Formula 1 put a Grand Prix on a new Madrid circuit five days after Monza. New tracks scramble the form book. A championship leader with 267 points is supposed to be careful. Kimi Antonelli won anyway.

## What actually happened

Formula1.com: Antonelli 1:34:23.754 from grid 2. Max Verstappen +4.351. Lando Norris +5.089 after starting on pole in 1:31.824. Charles Leclerc +29.116. George Russell +29.829 with the fastest lap, 1:35.587 on lap 49.

The Athletic: Norris led by 6.2 seconds on lap 14 when Lance Stroll stopped at Turn 20. The virtual safety car came out. Norris was too far around the circuit to pit. Antonelli was not. Norris recovered to hound Verstappen, locked his brakes on lap 55, and finished third.

Lewis Hamilton reported he had no brakes. He fell from fourth and retired on lap 6. Wikipedia lists the same DNF: Hamilton brakes, Stroll brakes, Sergio Perez water pressure, Carlos Sainz collision damage.

## What it changes

Drivers: Antonelli 292, Russell 211, Hamilton 191, Norris 186, Leclerc 167. Constructors: Mercedes 503, Ferrari 358, McLaren 306. Eighth win of the season. Lead 81 points. Next is Azerbaijan, 24-26 September. That one has not been raced.
