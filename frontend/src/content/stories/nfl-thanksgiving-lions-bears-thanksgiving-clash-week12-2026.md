America signature football holiday arrives with colossal divisional consequences on Thursday, 26 November, as the Detroit Lions host the Chicago Bears at Ford Field (1:00 PM ET, CBS). For only the fourth time across 193 all-time meetings, both Midwestern rivals enter Thanksgiving Day sporting identical 8-2 records.

Dan Campbell's Detroit side walks onto the Ford Field turf unbeaten at home and on a six-game overall winning streak, fresh off a 31-21 Week 11 win over Tampa Bay. Quarterback Jared Goff has orchestrated an efficient attack leaning on Jameson Williams and running back Jahmyr Gibbs, who punched in two touchdowns against the Buccaneers.

Waiting on the opposite sideline is former Lions offensive coordinator Ben Johnson, returning to Detroit as Chicago head coach on a five-game winning tear. Since returning from an earlier hamstring setback, quarterback Caleb Williams has steered the Bears through five straight contests without trailing for a single second, matching Chicago iconic 1985 stretch. Supported by receivers Rome Odunze and Luther Burden plus disruptive pass rushers Jeffery Simmons and Montez Sweat, Chicago arrives fighting for sole control of the NFC North ahead of Thursday nightcap between Patrick Mahomes Chiefs (8-2) and Josh Allen AFC-leading Bills (9-1).
