## Why this one matters

Through six rounds of the 2026-27 Premier League campaign, Pep Guardiola's Manchester City have separated themselves from the chasing pack. Saturday's hard-fought 2-1 victory over Newcastle United at the Etihad Stadium pushed City to an unblemished 18 points, establishing an early three-point cushion over second-placed Arsenal (15 pts) and six points over third-placed Liverpool (12 pts).

With UEFA Champions League Matchday 2 scheduled for Tuesday and Wednesday, City's domestic form provides crucial breathing room. Erling Haaland continues to redefine modern goalscoring standards, netting his 10th league goal in just six matches, while Rodri and Bernardo Silva control tempos with clinical precision.

## The state of the table

PremierLeague.com confirmed the official table following Sunday's fixtures:

Arsenal remain City's most persistent pursuers, moving to 15 points (5-0-1) following a composed 2-0 home victory against Everton at Emirates Stadium. Mikel Arteta's defense has conceded just four goals in six matches, keeping four clean sheets.

Liverpool sit in third place on 12 points (4-0-2) after rebounding with a 3-1 victory over Sunderland at the Stadium of Light, sparked by Mohamed Salah's brace. Fabian Hürzeler's Brighton occupy fourth with 11 points following their spirited 1-1 draw against Enzo Maresca's Chelsea (5th on 10 pts) at the Amex.

Further down the table, Brentford and Leeds United hold steady on 9 points, while Everton and Newcastle round out the top ten with 8 points apiece.

## The numbers that tell the story

- **City perfection:** 6 matches played, 6 wins, 15 goals scored, 6 conceded, 18 points.
- **Haaland pace:** 10 goals in 6 league matches, leading the Golden Boot race by five goals.
- **Arsenal defense:** 4 goals conceded in 6 matches, best defensive record in the top flight.
- **Top five:** Man City (18), Arsenal (15), Liverpool (12), Brighton (11), Chelsea (10).
- **European pivot:** Champions League Matchday 2 kicks off Tuesday across the continent.
