## Why this preview matters

The US Open is closed. Alexander Zverev beat Ben Shelton. Elena Rybakina beat Aryna Sabalenka. Wimbledon already had names. The next printed team tennis date is Laver Cup at The O2 in London.

## What we can print

Sky Sports: Friday 25 September to Sunday 27 September, The O2. Wikipedia, rankings as of 14 September: Team Europe is Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Flavio Cobolli, Rafael Jódar, Jakub Menšík, Casper Ruud. Team World is Brandon Nakashima, Alex de Minaur, Taylor Fritz, Learner Tien, Alexander Bublik, Francisco Cerúndolo. Shelton withdrew 17 September. Tommy Paul withdrew 12 September. Captains: Yannick Noah and Andre Agassi.

Format, from Sky and LaverCup.com: Friday wins one point, Saturday two, Sunday three. First to 13. A 12-12 tie would need a decider. BBC's tennis calendar also lists Hangzhou and Korea Open from 21 September. Those draws are not this page.

## What we will not print

A 2026 Laver Cup champion. A Friday singles score. A doubles pairing that has not been named for match day. Come back after 25 September.
