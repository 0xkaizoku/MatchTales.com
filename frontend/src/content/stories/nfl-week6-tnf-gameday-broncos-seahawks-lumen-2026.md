## Gameday at Lumen Field

The NFL launches Week 6 tonight in the Pacific Northwest as the Seattle Seahawks host the Denver Broncos at Lumen Field. Kickoff is scheduled for 8:15 p.m. ET (5:15 p.m. PT) with nationwide broadcast coverage on Amazon Prime Video and NFL+.

Seattle enter the prime time matchup at 4-1, buoyed by efficient quarterback play from Sam Darnold and consistent production on the ground from running back Kenneth Walker III. The Seahawks have won four of their first five games to stay in direct contention with the unbeaten San Francisco 49ers (5-0) in the NFC West.

Denver arrive at 2-3 after encountering narrow defeats against Kansas City and San Francisco. Rookie quarterback Bo Nix faces the formidable test of managing communication amidst Seattle's renowned home crowd noise.

## The league landscape entering Week 6

Week 5 closed Monday night in dramatic fashion when Tyler Bass converted a 45-yard field goal in overtime to seal Buffalo's 37-34 victory over the Los Angeles Rams at SoFi Stadium.

Three undefeated franchises remain in the league:

- Minnesota Vikings (5-0)
- San Francisco 49ers (5-0)
- Kansas City Chiefs (4-0, returning from their bye)

Both Buffalo and Baltimore stand at 4-1. A Seattle victory tonight would elevate the Seahawks to 5-1 and intensify the race for conference positioning.
