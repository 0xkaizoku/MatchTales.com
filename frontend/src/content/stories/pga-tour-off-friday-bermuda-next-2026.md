## Friday has no PGA Tour card

16 October is a blank day on the PGA Tour. There is no first-round leader, no cut, and no 18-hole total to print. Michael Brennan's win at the Baycurrent Classic still stands: 16 under, closing 68, 72-hole total 268. That is the last completed PGA Tour title on this desk.

## Bermuda is a date, not a score

The next PGA Tour event is the Butterfield Bermuda Championship, 22-25 October, at Port Royal Golf Course. The purse is 6 million dollars. Adam Schenk is the previous winner. He closed with a 71 in the wind and won by one. A commitment list is not a leaderboard. This desk does not attach a Friday score to a tournament that tees off next Thursday.
