## The state of the Premier League table

Manchester City lead the Premier League table with a perfect 18 points from six matches (6-0-0, 16 goals scored, 5 conceded). Pep Guardiola's side secured their three-point lead over Arsenal (15 points, 5-0-1) following Sunday's 3-0 victory over Liverpool at Anfield, where Erling Haaland, Jeremy Doku, and Rayan Cherki scored in the second half.

Brighton sit third with 13 points (4-1-1, 18 goals scored). Brentford, Everton, and Chelsea follow in fourth, fifth, and sixth with 10 points each. Liverpool are ninth on 9 points, level with Leeds United and Hull City. Tottenham Hotspur remain in the relegation zone in 19th on 3 points, with Fulham 20th on 2 points.

## Monday night wrap and Matchweek 7 outlook

Matchweek 6 completed on Monday at the Coventry Building Society Arena. Coventry City defeated Newcastle United 4-1 with goals from Jack Rudoni (penalty, 6th minute), Sidiki Cherif (48th), Sven Botman (70th), Ellis Simms (81st), and Stephen Mfuni (86th). The victory was Coventry's first home Premier League win in 25 years under manager Frank Lampard, lifting Coventry to 14th with 6 points. Newcastle stay 11th on 8 points.

Premier League action resumes on Saturday 17 October for Matchweek 7. This desk does not print scores for fixtures that have not kicked off.
