Midnight drama arrives in the Mojave Desert as the 2026 Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix prepares to roar into life tonight, Saturday 21 November, at 22:00 local time (01:00 ET Sunday).

The high-speed 6.201 km Strip circuit produced stunning qualifying pace. Although newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli posted the quickest benchmark lap for Mercedes, an unserved five-place grid penalty drops the teenage sensation back to sixth on the starting grid. That elevates Red Bull driver Max Verstappen to pole position, lining up alongside Mercedes driver George Russell on the front row.

Behind them, Charles Leclerc occupies third for Ferrari next to McLaren driver Lando Norris on row two. With Mercedes already sealing the Constructors Championship on 652 points, the ferocious five-way duel for runner-up in the Drivers standings takes center stage: Verstappen (279) holds a narrow 17-point lead over Russell (262), with Lewis Hamilton (261), Leclerc (249), and Norris (246) all within striking distance across 50 intense laps under the neon glare.
