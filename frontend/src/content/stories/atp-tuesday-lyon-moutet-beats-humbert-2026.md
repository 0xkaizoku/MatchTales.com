## Lyon on Tuesday

Corentin Moutet beat Ugo Humbert 6-2, 6-7(5), 7-6(4) in 3 hours 21 minutes at the ATP 250 in Lyon. The ATP match report is dated 20 October 2026. Humbert was the No. 7 seed and a two-time champion of this event when it was played in Marseille. Moutet, ranked 94, said he played with his heart. It was his first match since he retired from his opening match at the US Open. ATP lists his next opponent as Felix Balshaw. Also on the ATP Tuesday card: Borna Gojo beat Arthur Fery 6-3, 7-6(2), and Pierre-Hugues Herbert beat Sebastian Baez 6-3, 6-4.
