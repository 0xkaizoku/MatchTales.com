## Why this map matters

If you went to sleep after the World Cup final and woke up on 8 September, the sports year did not pause. It changed clothes. Here is the honest map.

## July

World Cup final, 19 July: Spain 1-0 Argentina aet, Torres 106'. Bronze, 18 July: England 6-4 France, Saka hat-trick. Wimbledon: Noskova 11 July, Sinner 12 July. The Open, 16-19 July: Ryan Fox 270. British GP 5 July, Leclerc. Belgian GP 19 July, Antonelli. Hungarian GP 26 July, Norris.

## August

Premier League opens 21 August. Hull 2-0 United. US Open mixed doubles 26 August, Muchova/Mensik. US Open singles begin 30 August. Tour Championship 27-30 August, Scheffler. Dutch GP 23 August, Norris.

## September, so far

Italian GP 6 September, Antonelli from 19th. Premier League matchweek 3 complete 6 September, City and Arsenal on 9, Hull on 7. US Open quarter-finals 8-9 September, still live.
