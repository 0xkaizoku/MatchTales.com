## Cinderella triumphs in both conferences

The 2026 WNBA postseason delivered a seismic changing of the guard on Sunday afternoon. In two breathtaking Game 4 battles, the Atlanta Dream and the Golden State Valkyries eliminated the reigning champion New York Liberty and Las Vegas Aces, closing out both best-of-five semifinal series 3-1 to book a dream Finals matchup that nobody predicted.

In Brooklyn, the Dream withstood a furious fourth-quarter charge from Breanna Stewart and Sabrina Ionescu to edge the Liberty 85-83 at Barclays Center. Rhyne Howard delivered 24 points while Angel Reese pulled down 16 rebounds, sending Atlanta to the WNBA Finals for the first time since 2013. Hours later in Las Vegas, the expansion Valkyries made sports history: Tiffany Hayes hit a step-back three in overtime as Golden State edged the Aces 87-83 at Michelob ULTRA Arena, becoming the first expansion team in professional basketball history to reach the Finals in its debut season.

## Game 1 looms in College Park

The stage is now officially set for the 2026 WNBA Finals between Atlanta and Golden State, tipping off Thursday, October 15 at Gateway Center Arena. With both defending powerhouses vanquished, women's basketball prepares to crown a brand-new champion.
