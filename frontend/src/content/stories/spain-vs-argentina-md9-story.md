## Why this match matters

A World Cup final is the only match that can end a month. This one ended with Spain as champions for the first time since 2010, Argentina one goal short of back-to-back titles, and a substitute on the scoresheet.

## What actually happened

19 July 2026, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford. Spain 1-0 Argentina after extra time. Ferran Torres scored in the 106th minute from a Nico Williams header. Torres had come on in the 62nd minute. He was named man of the match.

Regulation finished 0-0, the first goalless World Cup final since 1994. CBS reported Spain outshooting Argentina 20-3. FIFA said 20 attempts to two over 120 minutes. Argentina, reduced to ten after Enzo Fernandez was sent off, did not register a shot in normal time.

Emiliano Martinez made 11 saves, a World Cup final record, and still lost. Attendance 80,663. Referee Slavko Vincic of Slovenia. A brawl broke out after the whistle.

Spain conceded one goal in eight matches, a record for a champion. They became the first nation to hold the men's and women's World Cups at the same time.
