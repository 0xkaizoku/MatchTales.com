The Houston Texans sent a thunderous message across the AFC South on Thursday night, dominating division rivals Indianapolis Colts 26-3 in front of a raucous crowd of 71,842 at NRG Stadium.

Quarterback C.J. Stroud was surgical, completing 22 of 31 passes for 248 yards and two touchdowns, finding Nico Collins in the first quarter and rookie tight end Cade Stover in the third. Meanwhile, DeMeco Ryans relentless defense harassed Colts quarterback Anthony Richardson all evening, picking off two passes, forcing a fumble, and sacking the young passer four times while limiting Indianapolis to just 112 passing yards.

With the victory, Houston improves to 7-4, seizing clear pole position in the AFC South and sweeping the season series over Indianapolis (now 5-6). The NFL Week 11 spotlight now shifts to Sunday marquee clashes, including Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs visiting Carolina, and the San Francisco 49ers travelling to Lambeau Field to face the Green Bay Packers.
