## Why this one matters

Saturday brought a deceptive quiet across the WNBA landscape as the four remaining title contenders completed film study and shootarounds ahead of Sunday's massive Game 1 doubleheader.

In Minneapolis, the No. 1 overall seed Minnesota Lynx are gearing up for the league's hottest ticket: Caitlin Clark and the No. 4 Indiana Fever. After sweeping the Atlanta Dream in commanding fashion, Indiana enters Target Center brimming with confidence. Clark set a rookie playoff record with 51 points across the two games, while Kelsey Mitchell added 38 points of perimeter scoring.

However, Cheryl Reeve's Lynx represent a vastly superior defensive puzzle. With Defensive Player of the Year Napheesa Collier, versatile forward Alanna Smith, and lockdown guard Bridget Carleton, Minnesota posted the league's lowest defensive rating. How Indiana handles Minnesota's aggressive hedge-and-recover schemes will dictate whether the Fever can steal home-court advantage.

## Aces vs Liberty: The modern classic

In New York, Barclays Center hosts the latest chapter in what has become the defining rivalry of 2020s basketball: the Las Vegas Aces versus the New York Liberty.

The two franchises split the last two championships, with Las Vegas winning in 2023 and New York seeking redemption. Unanimous three-time MVP A'ja Wilson was virtually unguardable in the opening round, posting 28 points, 13 rebounds, and four blocks against Dallas.

To counter Wilson, Sandy Brondello's Liberty rely on frontcourt length with Jonquel Jones and Breanna Stewart. Sabrina Ionescu's shot creation from the perimeter gave New York the edge in their sweep of Golden State, and her matchup against Chelsea Gray will decide pace of play.

Sunday begins a best-of-five gauntlet where execution under intense pressure will crown the two teams destined for the 2026 WNBA Finals.
