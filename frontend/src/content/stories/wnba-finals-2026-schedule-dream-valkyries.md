## The week between the sweeps and the tip

Tuesday has no WNBA game. The Finals are a best-of-seven, and Game 1 is still upcoming. Golden State host Atlanta at Chase Center on Saturday 17 October at 3:30 p.m. ET, which is 12:30 p.m. PT, on NBC and Peacock.

The rest of the series, on the dates Sporting News, StatMuse and NBC list together: Game 2 is Monday 19 October at 8:00 p.m. ET at Chase Center on USA and Peacock. Game 3 is Thursday 22 October at 8:00 p.m. ET in Atlanta on USA and Peacock. Game 4 is Saturday 24 October at 3:30 p.m. ET in Atlanta on NBC and Peacock. If the series needs them: Game 5 is Monday 26 October at 8:00 p.m. ET in Golden State, Game 6 is Thursday 29 October at 8:00 p.m. ET in Atlanta, and Game 7 is Saturday 31 October at 8:00 p.m. ET in Golden State.

## What is already decided

Both semifinals ended 3-0. The Dream beat the Liberty 85-83 in New York, with Angel Reese scoring 22 points. The Valkyries beat the defending champion Aces 87-77 in overtime at Michelob ULTRA Arena. This desk does not print 87-83 or 88-77 for that clincher. The Valkyries are in year two. The Dream are in the Finals for the first time since 2013. Game 1 has no score.
