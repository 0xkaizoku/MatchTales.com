Week 11 of the NFL campaign launches under the prime-time spotlight on Thursday night, 19 November, as the Houston Texans welcome divisional rivals the Indianapolis Colts to NRG Stadium for a pivotal AFC South showdown.

The matchup features two of the most electrifying young quarterbacks in football. Houston's C.J. Stroud has orchestrated an efficient passing attack, connecting consistently with Nico Collins and Tank Dell while protecting the football with only four interceptions all year. On defense, the Texans front seven has terrorized opposing backfields, led by edge rusher Will Anderson Jr., who tops the AFC with 10 sacks.

Indianapolis presents a dynamic counterpunch behind Anthony Richardson, whose physical rushing threat and arm strength present unique defensive challenges. Supported by running back Jonathan Taylor, who enters Thursday averaging over five yards per carry, the Colts aim to establish early ground dominance and control the tempo.

With both teams firmly entrenched in the crowded AFC playoff hunt, Thursday's outcome delivers critical head-to-head tiebreaker leverage. The contest follows an unforgettable Week 10 that concluded on Monday with Jim Harbaugh's Chargers edging brother John's Ravens 23-20 in Baltimore.
