## Mid-October pause on the PGA Tour

The PGA Tour calendar features no sanctioned tournament play during the week of 13 to 19 October 2026. Following back-to-back tournaments in Utah and Japan, the FedExCup Fall schedule provides players with a brief respite before the final three-event international swing begins.

Michael Brennan's breakthrough victory at the Baycurrent Classic in Yokohama, Japan stands as the most recent official result. Brennan closed with a final-round 68 to finish at 16-under 268, collecting the winner's check of $1.44 million.

## Looking ahead to Port Royal

Competition resumes next week with the Butterfield Bermuda Championship, scheduled for 22 to 25 October at the par-71 Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda.

The event carries a total prize purse of $6 million, with Adam Schenk listed as the defending champion. Preliminary field commitments released on Thursday highlight a competitive mix of tour veterans and emerging contenders seeking to solidify their top-125 FedExCup status for the 2027 campaign.

No competitive rounds have commenced in Bermuda, and the tournament board remains empty until Thursday 22 October.
