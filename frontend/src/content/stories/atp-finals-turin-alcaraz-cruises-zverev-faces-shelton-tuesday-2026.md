Carlos Alcaraz made a resounding opening statement at the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals in Turin on Monday, 16 November, sweeping aside close friend Frances Tiafoe 6-2, 6-2 in just 69 minutes of scintillating tennis at Inalpi Arena.

The Spanish World No. 2 produced near-flawless numbers from the baseline, firing 24 winners against only seven unforced errors and never facing a break point on his own serve. Alcaraz targeted Tiafoe's second serve with relentless aggressive returns, breaking the American four times in total to sprint to the top of the Ivan Lendl Group standings. In the night match, Daniil Medvedev survived an electric atmosphere to defeat Italian home hope Flavio Cobolli 7-6 (4), 6-3, using clinical tiebreak depth to take control.

Attention shifts back to the John McEnroe Group on Tuesday, 17 November, with a blockbuster night match featuring World No. 1 Alexander Zverev against American star Ben Shelton. The clash is a high-stakes rematch of September's US Open final, where Zverev prevailed in four sets to capture his third Grand Slam title. Both players enter Tuesday at 1-0, meaning the winner will take sole possession of first place and virtually ensure passage to the weekend semi-finals.

In Tuesday's afternoon match, France's Arthur Fils and Spain's Rafael Jodar square off at 2:00 p.m. CET in an elimination battle where neither player can afford an 0-2 hole in round-robin play.
