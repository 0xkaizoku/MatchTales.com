The Orlando Magic authored the biggest upset of Monday night, 16 November, storming back from an eight-point deficit in the fourth quarter to stun the Boston Celtics 115-105 before a stunned TD Garden crowd.

Boston appeared poised to extend its six-game winning streak after Jayson Tatum scored 28 points to build a 84-81 advantage through three quarters. But Orlando completely flipped the script in the final 12 minutes with aggressive blitzing defense and flawless perimeter execution. Sharpshooter Desmond Bane took over the game, scoring 14 of his team-high 24 points in the fourth quarter, highlighted by three consecutive three-pointers that ignited a game-breaking 18-4 run.

Franz Wagner supplied 22 points and seven assists for Orlando, while Paolo Banchero controlled the boards with 18 points and 11 rebounds. Defensively, the Magic held Boston to just 39 percent shooting from the field in the second half, limiting Jaylen Brown and Tatum to a combined two field goals over the final eight minutes of action.

With the win, Orlando climbs to 8-5 and solidifies its status as a top-four Eastern Conference threat. Across the league on Monday, the Miami Heat picked up their fifth consecutive victory in a 128-110 rout of the Houston Rockets behind Giannis Antetokounmpo (20 points, 13 rebounds), while Joel Embiid dropped 37 points in Philadelphia's 135-123 victory over the Brooklyn Nets.
