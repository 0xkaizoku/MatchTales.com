## Why this tournament matters

Following Alexander Zverev and Elena Rybakina lifting trophies at Flushing Meadows, global tennis shifts immediately to the iconic black court at The O2 in London. The 9th edition of the Laver Cup marks a historic transition: legendary captains Bjorn Borg and John McEnroe have stepped aside, passing the leadership torches to Yannick Noah and Andre Agassi.

## What actually happened

Laver Cup organizers and Sky Sports confirmed Thursday's pre-tournament press conferences and squad announcements. Team Europe enters seeking redemption behind US Open champion Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Casper Ruud, Flavio Cobolli, Jakub Mensik, and Spanish prodigy Rafael Jodar.

Agassi's Team World squad features a resilient core despite late roster changes. US Open finalist Taylor Fritz and Alex de Minaur headline the lineup, flanked by Brandon Nakashima (replacing Ben Shelton), Learner Tien, Alexander Bublik, and Francisco Cerundolo.

Under the Laver Cup points progression system, matches on Friday award one point each, Saturday two points, and Sunday three points. The first team to reach 13 points claims the cup. Noah and Agassi confirmed three singles matches and one doubles tie for Friday's opening day.

## What to watch next

The opening day matches begin Friday 25 September at 1:00 p.m. UK time at The O2. Can Agassi guide Team World to another away victory, or will Noah inspire Europe to reclaim supremacy on London's premier indoor stage?
