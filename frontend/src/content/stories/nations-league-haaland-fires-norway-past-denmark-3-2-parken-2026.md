The UEFA Nations League group stage concluded in breathtaking fashion on Tuesday night, 17 November, as Erling Haaland delivered an 88th-minute winner to give Norway a sensational 3-2 victory over Scandinavian rivals Denmark at Parken Stadium in Copenhagen.

The Nordic clash lived up to its intense billing from the outset. Rasmus Hojlund put the hosts ahead early, but Norway responded before halftime when Alexander Sorloth powered home a commanding header. Rising star Antonio Nusa gave Norway a 2-1 lead in the 61st minute with a spectacular curling strike, before Danish talisman Christian Eriksen brought the stadium to its feet with a trademark dipping free kick in the 74th minute.

With the contest hanging in the balance, Norwegian captain Martin Odegaard unlocked the Danish defense with a pinpoint through-ball. Haaland held off his marker with trademark strength, rounded the keeper, and finished calmly with his left foot to claim Norway's first competitive win on Danish soil in over three decades.

The dramatic 3-2 scoreline put the finishing touches on the Nations League autumn calendar, following Germany's 5-0 demolition of the Netherlands on Monday. Attention now shifts back to domestic club football, with the Premier League resuming on Saturday, 21 November, where leaders Arsenal (27 points) defend a five-point cushion over Brighton (22) and Manchester City (21).
