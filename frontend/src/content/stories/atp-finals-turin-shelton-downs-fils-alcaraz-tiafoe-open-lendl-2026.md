The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals in Turin produced high drama on Sunday night, 15 November, as American third seed Ben Shelton survived an absorbing battle against France's Arthur Fils, claiming a 6-2, 3-6, 6-3 victory at Inalpi Arena. The result capped an exhilarating opening day in the John McEnroe Group following Alexander Zverev's 6-4, 6-4 afternoon victory over Spain's Rafael Jodar.

Shelton established early command with blistering serving, firing seven aces in a dominant first set to break Fils twice. The Frenchman responded with ferocious baseline hitting in the second set, neutralizing Shelton's kick serve and capitalizing on a crucial break at 3-2 to force a deciding set. In the third, Shelton elevated his game, unleashing four unreturnable serves to fend off two break points at 2-2 before securing the decisive break in the sixth game. Shelton finished with 16 aces and 38 winners in two hours and eight minutes, joining Zverev atop the McEnroe Group standings with a 1-0 record.

The focus in northern Italy shifts to the Ivan Lendl Group on Monday, 16 November. World No. 2 Carlos Alcaraz takes center stage against close friend and rival Frances Tiafoe not before 2:00 p.m. CET, followed by former champion Daniil Medvedev taking on Italian favorite Flavio Cobolli in the evening session. Alcaraz arrives in Turin determined to capture his maiden season finale crown, but Tiafoe's aggressive hard-court style promises a demanding opening test.
