## A quiet Tuesday on a loud championship

There is no Grand Prix on 13 October. The drivers' table after Singapore is unchanged. Kimi Antonelli has 340 points. George Russell has 238. The gap is 102. Lewis Hamilton has 229, Max Verstappen 221, Charles Leclerc 215 and Lando Norris 201. Oscar Piastri has 138.

The constructors' table is also unchanged. Mercedes have 578. Ferrari have 444, a gap of 134. McLaren have 339, which is the team total, not Antonelli's. Red Bull have 332.

## Austin, then the rest of the flyaways

Formula1.com lists the United States Grand Prix at the Circuit of the Americas for 23-25 October 2026, with the race on 25 October. The full timetable lists the grand prix at 15:00 local time and notes that the timetable can change. This desk does not convert that clock into US Eastern time. The circuit page lists 5.513 km, 56 laps and a race distance of 308.405 km. The first United States Grand Prix there was in 2012. The same page lists a historical fastest lap of 1:36.169 by Charles Leclerc in 2019. No session in Austin has a result.

The official calendar after Austin: Mexico 30 October to 1 November, Brazil 6-8 November, Las Vegas 19-21 November, Qatar 27-29 November, Abu Dhabi 4-6 December.
