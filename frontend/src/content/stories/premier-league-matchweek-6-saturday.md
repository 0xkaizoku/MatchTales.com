## Why this matchweek matters

The Premier League calendar reaches Matchweek 6 on Saturday 26 September with Manchester City standing alone as the only club with five victories from five matches. With Arsenal, Brighton, and a congested chasing pack within striking distance, Saturday's action will shape the autumn title landscape.

## What actually happened

PremierLeague.com and Sky Sports outline the fixtures across England. At the Etihad Stadium, Pep Guardiola's leaders host Newcastle United in the teatime kickoff. City enter with 15 points following an explosive 5-3 victory over Sunderland, powered by Antoine Semenyo's double and Erling Haaland's clinical finishing. Haaland has scored in every league fixture this term, posing a formidable challenge for Matthias Jaissle's defense.

Newcastle arrive in ninth place on eight points, aiming to bounce back from recent road struggles. Jaissle has tasked Alexander Isak and Anthony Gordon with exploiting transitional spaces behind City's high defensive line.

Elsewhere in Saturday's schedule, Arsenal host Everton at Emirates Stadium seeking a statement response following their 3-0 defeat at Brighton. Chelsea face Fabian Hurzeler's third-placed Brighton at Stamford Bridge, while Liverpool travel to Anfield seeking three points against Sunderland.

## What to watch next

Can Newcastle become the first club to breach City's winning fortress in 2026, or will Guardiola's men march to six consecutive wins before next week's Champions League fixtures?
