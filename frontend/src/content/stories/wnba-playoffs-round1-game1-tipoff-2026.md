## Why this Sunday matters

Sunday 27 September launches the 2026 WNBA playoffs with four consecutive Game 1 contests across America. Under the best-of-three opening round format, the team that captures Game 1 wins the series nearly eighty percent of the time, putting immense pressure on every single possession from the opening tip.

## What actually happened

WNBA.com and ESPN report that action begins at Target Center, where the top-seeded Minnesota Lynx (32-10) host the eighth-seeded Washington Mystics. Minnesota relies on the league's most suffocating perimeter defense to counter Washington's fast-break scoring.

In the afternoon window, Caitlin Clark and the fifth-seeded Indiana Fever (27-15) invade Gateway Center Arena to face the fourth-seeded Atlanta Dream (28-14). Clark finished the regular season leading the league in assists and three-pointers, and this series promises to draw record-setting television viewership.

The evening slate moves to the West Coast. The second-seeded Golden State Valkyries (31-11) make their franchise playoff debut at Chase Center against Sabrina Ionescu and the seventh-seeded New York Liberty. Finally, A'ja Wilson and the defending champion Las Vegas Aces (29-13) host Arike Ogunbowale and the Dallas Wings in Las Vegas.

## What to watch next

Four games in eight hours will establish the tone for the entire 2026 postseason. Can lower seeds steal a road victory, or will home favorites take commanding 1-0 leads?
