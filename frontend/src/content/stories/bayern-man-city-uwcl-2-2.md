## Why this game matters

The UEFA Women's Champions League opened its 2026-27 league phase with a blockbuster pairing in Bavaria. With both clubs harboring genuine European title ambitions, Matchday 1 offered an immediate benchmark of continental quality.

## What actually happened

UEFA.com and The Guardian recount a contest rich in momentum swings. Bayern struck early when Giulia Gwinn fired home in the 11th minute, and the hosts doubled their advantage in the 28th minute after an unfortunate Kerstin Casparij own goal. City faced further adversity when Khadija Shaw saw her penalty kick turned aside by Bayern goalkeeper Ena Mahmutovic.

Undeterred by the deficit, City found a lifeline just before halftime when Beth Mead converted from close range. In the second half, City applied continuous pressure, ultimately rewarded in the 86th minute when substitute Carlotta Wamser beat Mahmutovic to cap an exhilarating 2-2 deadlock.

## What it changes

Both teams depart with one hard-fought point in the standings, setting up a fascinating race in the group phase. For City, returning from two goals behind away from home represents a massive psychological lift as their European campaign gets underway.
