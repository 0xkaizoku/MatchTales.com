## Monday night is still unplayed

The last game of NFL Week 6 is Monday, 19 October, at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara. The Washington Commanders (1-3-1) visit the San Francisco 49ers (5-0). ESPN lists kickoff at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN and ABC. This desk does not post a score. The game has not been played.

San Francisco is one of three unbeaten teams, with the Kansas City Chiefs and the Minnesota Vikings. The Vikings are on a bye at 5-0. CBS Sports listed the 49ers as 7.5-point favorites on the Week 6 Monday line.

## Sunday's finals, from the ESPN scoreboard

Arrowhead finished Los Angeles Chargers 6, Kansas City Chiefs 22. Quarters: Chargers 3, 0, 3, 0 and Chiefs 9, 10, 0, 3. ESPN lists Patrick Mahomes with 272 passing yards, Kenneth Walker III with 128 rushing yards and Xavier Worthy with 73 receiving yards. The Chiefs are 5-0. The Chargers are 0-6.

At Allegiant Stadium the Buffalo Bills beat the Las Vegas Raiders 33-30. Quarters: Bills 3, 10, 10, 10 and Raiders 7, 6, 0, 17. The Bills are 5-1. The Raiders are 3-3. StatMuse reported that Josh Allen tied Jim Kelly's Bills record for touchdown passes.

Sunday night at Lambeau Field ended Dallas Cowboys 13, Green Bay Packers 23. Thursday night still stands: Denver Broncos 17, Seattle Seahawks 14 in overtime. The Broncos are 4-2. The Seahawks are 3-3. The Associated Press Monday glance also has the New England Patriots at 4-2 and the Jacksonville Jaguars at 4-2.
