## Three undefeated records face Sunday road tests

Week 7 of the 2026 NFL regular season reaches its crescendo on Sunday, 25 October, with three unblemished franchises putting their perfect seasons on the line across an action-packed 12-game slate. The headliner arrives in prime time on Sunday Night Football, where Patrick Mahomes and the defending champion Kansas City Chiefs (5-0) venture into the hostile noise of Lumen Field to take on the Seattle Seahawks.

Earlier in the afternoon, the NFL's winningest team, the San Francisco 49ers (6-0), travel to Mercedes-Benz Stadium to square off against the Atlanta Falcons. Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey lead a 49ers offense averaging over 29 points per contest, but Atlanta's pass rush represents a stiff road obstacle. Meanwhile in Minneapolis, the surprising Minnesota Vikings (5-0) host the Indianapolis Colts at U.S. Bank Stadium, aiming to keep pace at the top of the competitive NFC North.

## Bears ride TNF momentum as divisional battles loom

The foundation for Week 7 was set on Thursday night at Soldier Field, where the Chicago Bears improved to 5-2 with an exhilarating 30-24 victory over the New England Patriots. Caleb Williams passed for 308 yards and two touchdowns, while Cairo Santos booted a franchise-record-tying 58-yard field goal in the closing minutes to hand New England their third defeat (4-3).

Sunday's schedule features crucial divisional matchups elsewhere, including the Green Bay Packers visiting the Detroit Lions and the Baltimore Ravens clashing with the Cincinnati Bengals. With the Buffalo Bills (5-1) resting on their bye week, Sunday's results will reshape the playoff picture before Monday Night Football pits the Dallas Cowboys against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia.
