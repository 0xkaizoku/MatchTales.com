## Why this match matters

The powerhouse programs of women's basketball refused to be pushed to the brink. Facing potential 0-2 series holes after stunning Sunday defeats, the New York Liberty and Las Vegas Aces responded on Wednesday night with ruthless road composure to level their best-of-five semifinal series at 1-1. Both matchups now head to home arenas in Brooklyn and Las Vegas, transforming the postseason into a pair of high-stakes best-of-three sprints.

## What actually happened

In College Park, Georgia, the Liberty weathered a hostile Gateway Center Arena crowd to turn back the fourth-seeded Atlanta Dream 86-80. Sabrina Ionescu spearheaded the response with 26 points, including four clutch three-pointers, while Breanna Stewart anchored the interior with 21 points and 12 rebounds. Atlanta guard Jordin Canada scored 22 points, but New York executed down the stretch, using an 11-4 closing run to seal the split.

Three time zones away at Chase Center in San Francisco, the Las Vegas Aces silenced the noisy expansion Golden State Valkyries 83-74. A'ja Wilson delivered one of the defining performances of the postseason, racking up 28 points, 13 rebounds and 4 blocks. Kelsey Plum poured in 20 points as Becky Hammon's side adjusted to Golden State's switching defense and limited Gabby Williams to 19 points.

## What happens next

With both series tied at 1-1, home-court advantage returns to the heavyweights. Game 3 of Liberty vs Dream will take place Friday night at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, while the Aces host the Valkyries at Michelob ULTRA Arena in Las Vegas. Neither Atlanta nor Golden State could deliver the knockout blow, and now the champions have found their rhythm.
