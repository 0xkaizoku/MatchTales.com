## Why this Sunday matters

Thursday opened Week 2 in a new Highmark Stadium. Buffalo 41, Detroit 31. ESPN: Josh Allen five total touchdowns. That is the only printed Week 2 final. Sunday 20 September is a listed slate, not a box score.

## What is actually on the board

CBS and FOX: eight 1:00 p.m. ET games, including Steelers at Patriots, Bengals at Texans, Vikings at Bears, Packers at Jets, Eagles at Titans. Two 4:05 games: Jaguars at Broncos, Raiders at Chargers. Three 4:25 games: Seahawks at Cardinals, Dolphins at 49ers, Commanders at Cowboys. Sunday Night: Colts at Chiefs, 8:20 p.m. ET, NBC, Arrowhead. Monday Night: Giants at Rams, 8:15 p.m. ET, 21 September, ABC.

The Tennessean, FantasyPros and CBS all print times. None of those pages print a Sunday final.

## What we will not print

A Sunday winner. A Mahomes line. A Burrow box. A Monday Night score dated 20 September. Bills 41-31 is the locked result. The rest of Week 2 has not been played.
