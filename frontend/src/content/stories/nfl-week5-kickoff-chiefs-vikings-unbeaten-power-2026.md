## Why this match matters

Four weeks into the 2026 NFL season, thirty teams have felt the sting of defeat. Only the Kansas City Chiefs and Minnesota Vikings remain undefeated at 4-0, each surviving pulse-pounding fourth quarters on Sunday afternoon to protect their unblemished records. Patrick Mahomes orchestrated a vintage late drive in Las Vegas to edge the Raiders 30-27, while Brian Flores' defense in Minneapolis turned back Miami on the final snap to preserve a gritty 15-10 defensive clinic.

## What actually happened

The rest of the league enters Week 5 recalibrating after a chaotic Sunday and Monday. The Carolina Panthers generated the biggest shockwave of the weekend by ambushing the Detroit Lions 32-26 on Sunday Night Football, while the New Orleans Saints celebrated their twentieth Domecoming anniversary by clipping the Atlanta Falcons 26-24 on Monday night. Those results leave the middle tier of both conferences in utter gridlock.

## What happens next

Week 5 gets underway on Thursday night when the Falcons make the quick turnaround to visit Raymond James Stadium for an NFC South clash with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. With division margins tightening by the week, Thursday's prime-time encounter will set the tone for an October calendar where contender status is earned in the trenches.
