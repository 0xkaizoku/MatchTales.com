## Why this event matters

After JJ Spaun captured the BMW PGA Championship at Wentworth, the international golf calendar pivots to Chicago for one of team golf's premier showdowns. The 16th Presidents Cup opened Thursday morning at Medinah Country Club, testing 24 top golfers on a completely redesigned Course No. 3.

## What actually happened

PGA Tour and PresidentsCup.com report that captains Brandt Snedeker and Geoff Ogilvy revealed their Day 1 foursomes pairings following Wednesday's opening gala. Snedeker anchors his American squad with world No. 1 Scottie Scheffler pairing with Russell Henley in Match 1, followed by Xander Schauffele and Patrick Cantlay taking on Jason Day and Christiaan Bezuidenhout.

Ogilvy counters with deep International experience. Adam Scott makes history by teeing off in his record-equalling 12th consecutive Presidents Cup, teaming up with former Masters champion Hideki Matsuyama. Min Woo Lee and Tom Kim combine youthful flair in Match 3 against Collin Morikawa and Sahith Theegala, while Open champion Ryan Fox pairs with Taylor Pendrith against Sam Burns and Wyndham Clark.

The tactical battle centers on Medinah's revamped architecture. Ogilvy's own design firm, OCM Golf, redesigned Course No. 3, widening landing zones while creating treacherous runoff areas around newly contoured greens.

## What to watch next

Thursday foursomes award four critical points in the race to 15.5. Can the International side end an American winning streak stretching back to 1998, or will Team USA establish early dominance in Chicago?
