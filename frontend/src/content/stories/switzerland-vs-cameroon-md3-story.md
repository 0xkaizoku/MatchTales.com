Seattle. The fans at Lumen Field were treated to a proper tactical duel as Switzerland and Cameroon played out a hard-fought 1-1 draw. Both sides showed why they are notoriously difficult to beat in major tournaments.

Granit Xhaka orchestrated the play for Switzerland, keeping things clean and tidy in the center of the pitch. On the other side, Bryan Mbeumo was a constant menace for the Cameroonian attack, using his physical strength to worry the Swiss defenders.

Switzerland struck first through Breel Embolo, who tapped in a low cross. But Cameroon refused to lie down. Andre-Frank Zambo Anguissa drove his team forward, and they got their reward when Mbeumo found space in the box to smash home the equalizer past Andre Onana's counterpart. Both teams had chances to win it late on, but a draw was a fair result.
