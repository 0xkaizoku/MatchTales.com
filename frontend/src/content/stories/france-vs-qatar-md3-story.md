Houston. The crowd at NRG Stadium witnessed a footballing masterclass on June 24 as France cruised to a 3-0 win against Qatar. Les Bleus demonstrated their credentials as tournament favorites with a performance that was as ruthless as it was elegant.

Kylian Mbappe led from the front, scoring the opener with a trademark burst of pace and a composed finish. The Qatar team, captained by Hassan Al-Haydos, struggled to cope with the sheer physical presence of the French midfield, where Aurelien Tchouameni dictated play at his own pace.

Despite the scoreline, Qatar showed moments of promise. Akram Afif tried to spark life into the attack with some clever dribbling, but the French defensive line stayed secure. Goals from Mbappe, Tchouameni, and a late strike from substitute Desire Doue sealed the three points for France, sending a clear warning to their upcoming opponents.
