## Why this match matters

The October international window has reached its midpoint. In UEFA Nations League Group A4, Portugal became the first nation to guarantee a quarterfinal spot after defeating Norway 2-1 in Porto without Cristiano Ronaldo. Meanwhile in Group A2, Germany missed an opportunity to climb into second place after being held to a 0-0 draw by Greece in Munich. Across domestic leagues, the Premier League remains suspended until 10 October, with Manchester City retaining top spot on 15 points from five matches.

## What actually happened

Portugal's 2-1 victory over Norway at Estádio do Dragão saw Kristoffer Ajer open the scoring in the 36th minute before João Cancelo leveled two minutes later and Gonçalo Ramos netted the 79th-minute decider. Francisco Conceição received a late red card. In Munich, Julian Nagelsmann's Germany controlled 68 percent of possession but could not penetrate an organized Greek defense, settling for a scoreless draw. England's 7-0 demolition of Croatia in Rijeka and Spain's 3-1 triumph over Czechia in Oviedo stand verified on the international ledger.

## What it changes

Portugal sit on 12 points with a flawless 4-0-0 record in Group A4, out of reach for third-placed Norway (3 points). In Group A2, Germany remain in third position on 5 points, trailing Italy and France. Domestic club action remains on pause: Arsenal (12 points) and Brighton (10 points) trail Manchester City at the top of the English table until Matchweek 6 kicks off on Saturday 10 October.
