The most glamorous street circuit in motorsport comes alive on Thursday night, 19 November, as the Formula 1 field takes to the Las Vegas Strip for Free Practice 1 and Free Practice 2 ahead of Round 21 (19-21 November).

While Mercedes teenage prodigy Kimi Antonelli has already secured the 2026 Drivers' World Championship with 390 points, the battle for second place in the drivers' standings is wide open. Only 33 points separate second-placed Max Verstappen (279 points) from sixth-placed Lando Norris (246 points), with George Russell (262), Lewis Hamilton (261), and Charles Leclerc (249) sandwiched tightly between them.

The 6.201-kilometer layout down Las Vegas Boulevard features a 1.9-kilometer full-throttle straight where cars exceed 350 km/h under glittering hotel facades. With ambient desert temperatures expected to drop below 12 degrees Celsius during night sessions, tire warm-up and graining management will represent the critical technical hurdle for engineers.

In the Constructors' Championship, Ferrari (510 points) seeks to protect its 95-point margin over Red Bull (415) and McLaren (398) across the season's final triple-header. Thursday's opening 60-minute practice sessions provide the first real baseline data on track evolution and downforce balance.
