Formula 1 travels directly from the neon canyon of Nevada to the floodlit desert sweepers of Lusail International Circuit for Round 22, the 2026 Qatar Grand Prix, opening Friday, 27 November with Free Practice 1 (16:30 local AST) ahead of Sunday 57-lap night race (19:00 AST). Unlike previous seasons, Lusail returns to the traditional three-practice, qualifying, and Grand Prix format with no F1 Sprint scheduled.

Max Verstappen enters the penultimate round riding high from an instant classic in Las Vegas last weekend. Across 50 laps of active aerodynamic pack racing and 35 changes for the lead, the Red Bull star secured his 4th win of 2026 and 75th career Grand Prix victory by 0.639 seconds over Lando Norris. McLaren teammate Oscar Piastri stormed from 17th on the grid onto the final podium step after late chaos eliminated both factory Mercedes challengers: George Russell hit the barrier on Lap 48 (finishing 9th) and champion Kimi Antonelli suffered a sudden power loss while fighting up front.

Although Antonelli already owns the Drivers crown on 402 points and Mercedes hold the Constructors trophy on 666 points, huge stakes remain alive over the final two rounds. Verstappen consolidated second place at 304 points, while only three points separate third-placed Lewis Hamilton (267) from tied rivals Russell and Norris (264). In the Constructors table, surging McLaren (431) sit only nine points behind Red Bull (440) for third overall behind Ferrari (517).
