## Why this note matters

Sports websites invent minutes. We did that too, once, on a bronze-final story that gave Harry Kane a brace he did not score. FIFA had Saka with a hat-trick. We fixed the page. Then we wrote this.

## The rules

1. Score, date, venue, attendance, scorer and ranking must sit in a named source.
2. Primary sources first: FIFA, Premier League, ATP/WTA/usopen.org, PGA/R&A, NFL.com, NBA.com, WNBA.com, Formula 1 classifications via BBC/Wikipedia/ESPN when they agree.
3. If two reputable sources disagree on a detail, we do not print the detail. We print the score they share.
4. No invented quotes. No invented psychology. Texture comes from what happened and why the stake is real.
5. Every story ends with a Sources list.

## The clock

Today on this site is 8 September 2026. The World Cup ended 19 July. We will not pretend otherwise.
