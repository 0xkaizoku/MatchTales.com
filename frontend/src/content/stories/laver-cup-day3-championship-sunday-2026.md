## Why this finale matters

The 9th Laver Cup reaches Championship Sunday on 27 September under the roof of The O2 in London. Under the competition format, point allocations escalate to three points per win on Day 3, meaning any four-point deficit can flip in a single session. The first team to reach 13 points claims the cup.

## What actually happened

LaverCup.com and Sky Sports confirm the Sunday order of play submitted by team captains Yannick Noah and Andre Agassi. After two days of fierce momentum swings on the custom black court, both squads enter Sunday within striking distance of the title.

Match 9 kicks off the day with a marquee doubles showdown as Carlos Alcaraz and Alexander Zverev pair up for Team Europe against Taylor Fritz and Alex de Minaur for Team World. With three points riding on the doubles, the opening rubber will establish Sunday tempo.

Singles action follows immediately: Alcaraz faces Australian stalwart De Minaur in Match 10, while reigning US Open champion Zverev battles Fritz in Match 11. If the tie remains undecided, Casper Ruud is slated to face Brandon Nakashima in a decisive Match 12.

## What to watch next

With 12 points available across four matches, Sunday at The O2 is pure theater. Can Agassi's Team World claim victory on European soil, or will Noah's Team Europe capture the golden trophy in London?
