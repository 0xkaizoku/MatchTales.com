Boston. The summer sun at Gillette Stadium set the stage for an intriguing clash of styles. Algeria's tactical discipline clashed with the flamboyant speed of Jamaica, with Les Fennecs emerging victorious in a tight 1-0 win.

Jamaica brought their signature vibes and explosive pace to the game. Leon Bailey and Michail Antonio were dangerous on the counter, but they found it difficult to break through a well-organized Algerian defensive block led by Ismael Bennacer.

The only goal of the match came from the boot of Riyad Mahrez. The Algerian captain cut inside from the right and unleashed a trademark curling effort into the top corner. Jamaica threw everything forward in the final minutes, but Algeria managed to preserve their clean sheet.
