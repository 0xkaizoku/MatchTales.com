The battle for the final four at the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals reaches peak intensity on Thursday, 19 November, as the John McEnroe Group completes round-robin competition at Inalpi Arena in Turin.

World No. 1 Alexander Zverev has already navigated his passage into Saturday's semi-finals with two ruthless straight-sets victories, dispatching debutant Rafael Jodar 6-4, 6-4 before holding off American rival Ben Shelton 6-2, 7-6 (4) on Tuesday night. But behind the top-seeded German, the race for second place remains hanging by a thread.

Arthur Fils revived his tournament hopes on Tuesday afternoon by battling back from a set down against Jodar, claiming a gritty 4-6, 6-2, 6-2 win to level his record at 1-1. Today, Fils faces the formidable task of challenging Zverev in the evening session at 8:30 p.m. CET. Earlier at 2:00 p.m. CET, Shelton takes on the winless Jodar (0-2), knowing a straight-sets sweep gives him the inside track on percentage tiebreakers.

In Wednesday's Ivan Lendl Group action, Carlos Alcaraz produced sublime indoor hard-court form to dismiss Daniil Medvedev 6-2, 6-4 in 72 minutes without facing a single break point, joining Zverev in the semifinals with a pristine 2-0 record. With the weekend approaching, every single service game on Thursday carries monumental qualification weight.
