## Why this one matters

When the NFL schedule makers circled Sunday night of Week 4, they knew exactly what they were crafting: a colossal test between two genuine AFC heavyweights under the lights at M&T Bank Stadium.

The Buffalo Bills arrive in Maryland as the league's most balanced and dangerous outfit through three weeks. At 3-0, Buffalo leads the NFL in scoring at 34.3 points per game. Josh Allen has been magnificent under offensive coordinator Joe Brady, compiling 11 total touchdowns without throwing an interception or coughing up a fumble. Running back James Cook has provided thunder in the backfield, forcing defenses to defend every blade of grass.

For John Harbaugh and the Baltimore Ravens, Sunday night carries urgent stakes. After navigating a tough opening stretch to sit at 1-2, Baltimore cannot afford to fall two games below .500 in an unforgiving AFC North. Lamar Jackson remains the ultimate dual-threat weapon, while four-time rushing champion Derrick Henry has revitalized Baltimore's between-the-tackles identity.

## The tactical clash

Sunday night represents a clash of distinct football philosophies:

- **The turnover battle:** Buffalo enters Sunday with zero offensive turnovers, while Baltimore's defense has forced just two through three weeks.
- **Containing Lamar Jackson:** Buffalo's front four, led by Gregory Rousseau, must maintain lane discipline to prevent Jackson from breaking free on third downs.
- **Derrick Henry's downhill power:** The Ravens will test Buffalo's interior run defense early, seeking to control time of possession and keep Allen on the sideline.
- **Allen's deep-shot precision:** Without a singular target dominating target shares, Allen has distributed completions to Khalil Shakir, Dalton Kincaid, and Keon Coleman with devastating efficiency.

Following San Francisco's dramatic 24-20 victory over the Rams on Thursday Night Football, Sunday night in Baltimore serves as the definitive measuring stick for AFC supremacy.
