## The table has not moved since Singapore

RacingNews365's championship table after Singapore still reads: Andrea Kimi Antonelli 340, George Russell 238, Lewis Hamilton 229, Max Verstappen 221, Charles Leclerc 215, Lando Norris 201. The gap from Antonelli to Russell is 102 points. Constructors: Mercedes 578, Ferrari 444, McLaren 339, Red Bull 332. Mercedes lead Ferrari by 134. McLaren's 339 is a team total, not a driver total.

## Austin is listed. It has not been raced

The same calendar puts the United States Grand Prix on 25 October 2026, with the sprint weekend of 23-25 October at Circuit of the Americas. The race time stays 15:00 local. This desk does not convert that clock to Eastern Time. Friday 16 October is not a Formula 1 session day.

A results table that already names a United States Grand Prix winner is not used here. The race is still a week away. The Singapore result stands: Verstappen won from pole. Antonelli's 340 points already include that weekend.
