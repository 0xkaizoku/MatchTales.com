## Why this victory matters

Matchweek 6 confirmed Manchester City's undisputed supremacy in the autumn title race. Pep Guardiola's men outfought Newcastle United 2-1 on Saturday evening to become the only side in England with six wins from six fixtures, opening a three-point lead over second-placed Arsenal.

## What actually happened

PremierLeague.com and Sky Sports report that Erling Haaland opened the scoring in the 31st minute at the Etihad Stadium. The Norwegian superstar timed his run onto a pinpoint Kevin De Bruyne pass, slotting low past Nick Pope for his ninth league goal of the campaign. Haaland has now scored in every single league match this season.

Newcastle responded vigorously after the interval. In the 52nd minute, Alexander Isak found space behind City's defensive line to slide an equalizer inside the far post. But City refused to yield momentum. In the 68th minute, Bernardo Silva collected the ball on the edge of the penalty area and curled a sublime strike into the top corner.

Across Saturday's other fixtures, Arsenal kept pace with a 2-0 win over Everton at Emirates Stadium behind goals from Bukayo Saka and Kai Havertz, while Liverpool defeated Sunderland 3-1 at Anfield. Chelsea and Brighton battled to a 1-1 stalemate at Stamford Bridge.

## What to watch next

City enter next week's Champions League fixtures with supreme confidence and an 18-point league tally. Can anyone slow down Guardiola's side before the October international break?
