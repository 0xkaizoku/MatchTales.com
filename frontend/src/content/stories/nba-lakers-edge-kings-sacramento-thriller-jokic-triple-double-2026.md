Friday night NBA action delivered pure theatre across the Western Conference on 20 November, headlined by a nail-biting Pacific Division rivalry finish at Golden 1 Center in Sacramento.

The Los Angeles Lakers survived a ferocious fourth-quarter rally from the Sacramento Kings to escape with a 106-104 victory. Austin Reaves showed nerves of steel, sinking two free throws with 4.1 seconds remaining before DeAaron Fox contested buzzer-beating stepback three-pointer rimmed out. Fox finished with a game-high 29 points for the Kings, but Anthony Davis (24 points, 14 rebounds) and LeBron James (18 points, 9 assists) anchored Los Angeles down the stretch.

In Denver, three-time MVP Nikola Jokic orchestrated another masterpiece, compiling 32 points, 12 rebounds, and 10 assists to lead the Nuggets to a 133-124 victory over the young Houston Rockets. Combined with Cleveland 128-119 road victory in Indiana, Friday slate reaffirmed that the race for playoff positioning in both conferences is tighter than ever.
