## Why this international window matters

When club football steps aside in early October, international football often gets accused of slowing down momentum. This week, however, there is zero room for boredom.

From Rome to Santiago, national teams are wrestling with existential questions. Can Luciano Spalletti's bold new Italy solidify their rebirth after shocking France last month? Can a wounded Belgium survive without their golden generation generals? And most urgently of all, can Brazil halt their alarming slide in South American World Cup qualification before panic turns into catastrophe?

## The tactical battle in Rome

At the Stadio Olimpico on Thursday, Italy looks like a squad that has shed the tactical confusion of the summer. Spalletti has settled into an aggressive 3-5-1-1 shape built around Sandro Tonali, Samuele Ricci, and Davide Frattesi. Frattesi has been a revelation, timing his vertical bursts to score in both September victories over France and Israel.

Belgium travels to Rome in a far more delicate state. Domenico Tedesco is managing an acute talent transition, further complicated by the absence of Kevin De Bruyne and Romelu Lukaku. The creative weight falls squarely on Manchester City winger Jeremy Doku and RB Leipzig forward Lois Openda. If Belgium cannot find joy on the counter-attack, Italy's high press will suffocate them in front of a passionate home crowd.

## The storm brewing in South America

Across the Atlantic, CONMEBOL World Cup Qualifying Matchday 9 brings its own severe drama. World champions Argentina sit comfortably atop the standings with 18 points and welcome Lionel Messi back into the starting lineup against Venezuela.

For Brazil, the luxury of relaxation does not exist. The five-time world champions have suffered four defeats in their last five qualifiers, tumbling down to fifth place on ten points. Dorival Junior's side travels to Santiago to face Chile without Vinicius Junior, who suffered a neck injury playing for Real Madrid. A stumble against Chile would plunge Brazilian football into unprecedented qualifying panic, with direct qualification spots suddenly under genuine threat.
