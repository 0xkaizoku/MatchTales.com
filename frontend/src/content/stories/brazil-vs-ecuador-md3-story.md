Kansas City. The atmosphere at Arrowhead Stadium was nothing short of electric for this South American showdown. Brazil secured a narrow 2-1 win over a fiery Ecuador side, but they had to work incredibly hard for every single yard of turf.

Ecuador started the match with fearless energy. Young playmaker Kendry Paez showed maturity beyond his years, testing the Brazilian backline with his direct running and creativity. Behind him, Moises Caicedo was a midfield warrior, winning possession and setting the tempo for the underdogs.

Ecuador actually took the lead early on, catching Brazil off guard. But the Selecao responded like champions. Rodrygo equalized with a clinical finish before halftime, and in the second half, Vinicius Junior took matters into his own hands. The winger dribbled past three defenders before slipping the ball home, securing a precious victory for Brazil.
