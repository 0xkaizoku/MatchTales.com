## Week 6 opens in the Pacific Northwest

Week 6 of the 2026 NFL season begins Thursday 15 October at Lumen Field in Seattle. The Seattle Seahawks host the Denver Broncos on Amazon Prime Video and NFL+ with kickoff scheduled for 8:15 p.m. ET (5:15 p.m. PT).

Seattle enter Thursday night at 4-1 after victories over the Patriots (13-10), Cardinals (31-7), and Chargers (30-23). The Broncos arrive at 2-3 following losses to the Chiefs (31-10) and 49ers (24-14). Wednesday injury and practice reports showed both squads preparing for a physical contest in front of Seattle's 12th Man.

## Week 5 fallout and standings

Week 5 concluded on Monday night at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The Buffalo Bills defeated the Los Angeles Rams 37-34 in overtime. Tyler Bass kicked the winning 45-yard field goal with 1:55 remaining after Greg Rousseau blocked a Harrison Mevis attempt. Josh Allen totaled 331 yards and two touchdowns, lifting the Bills to 4-1 while the Rams dropped to 2-3.

Three teams remain undefeated across the NFL:
- Minnesota Vikings (5-0)
- San Francisco 49ers (5-0)
- Kansas City Chiefs (4-0, returning from their Week 5 bye)

The Baltimore Ravens stand 4-1 following Sunday night's 30-23 overtime win in Atlanta.
