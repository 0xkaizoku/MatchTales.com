## Why this match matters

The 2026 WNBA Semifinals resume Wednesday night with Game 2 double-header action that could tilt both series toward shocking conclusions. In the Eastern bracket, the fourth-seeded Atlanta Dream host the defending finalist New York Liberty at Gateway Center Arena. Atlanta stunned the basketball world on Sunday with a 92-82 Game 1 triumph, spearheaded by Jordin Canada's electric 25-point performance and DeWanna Bonner's veteran poise. New York must solve Atlanta's third-quarter blitzes to avoid flying back to Barclays Center facing a 0-2 deficit.

## What actually happened

Out West, the story is just as urgent. The expansion Golden State Valkyries, who produced a sensational 32-12 regular season to earn the second seed, welcome the two-time champion Las Vegas Aces to Chase Center in San Francisco. Golden State suffocated the Aces in Game 1, holding Becky Hammon's side to 60 points on 34 percent shooting behind a masterclass from Gabby Williams, who posted 25 points, 11 rebounds and 5 assists. A'ja Wilson battled for 24 points, but received negligible support from the Las Vegas bench.

## What happens next

In the history of best-of-five WNBA playoff series, teams that fall behind 0-2 have faced near-impossible odds of staging a comeback. For regular-season heavyweights New York and Las Vegas, Wednesday represents their fiercest gut-check of the campaign. For Atlanta and Golden State, forty disciplined minutes tonight would put them one win away from an improbable Finals showdown.
