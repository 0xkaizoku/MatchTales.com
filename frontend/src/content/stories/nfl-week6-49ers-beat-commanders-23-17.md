## Monday night is final

Levi's Stadium finished Washington Commanders 17, San Francisco 49ers 23. Quarters: Commanders 3, 0, 7, 7 and 49ers 13, 7, 3, 0. ESPN and the Associated Press both have that final. San Francisco is 6-0. Washington is 1-4-1.

Brock Purdy completed 21 of 29 passes for 264 yards and a touchdown, an 11-yard score to George Kittle. Christian McCaffrey rushed for two touchdowns, with 16 carries for 64 yards and nine receptions for 53 yards. Jayden Daniels completed 29 of 40 passes for 300 yards and two touchdowns. San Francisco led 20-3 at halftime and 23-3 late in the third quarter before Washington scored 14 unanswered points. FOX Sports listed the attendance at 71,561.

## Thursday is the next kickoff

There is no NFL game on Tuesday. Week 7 opens Thursday, 22 October, at 8:15 p.m. ET: New England Patriots at Chicago Bears, Soldier Field, on Prime Video. ESPN lists Chicago as a 3-point favorite. Schedule listings put both clubs at 4-2. The byes are the Bills, Jaguars, Chargers and Commanders. Sunday's earlier finals still stand: Chiefs 22, Chargers 6, and Bills 33, Raiders 30. The Chiefs and Vikings are 5-0.
