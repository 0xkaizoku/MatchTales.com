The 2026 PGA Tour FedExCup Fall season concluded with high drama on Monday morning, 23 November, as England Marco Penge survived a historic four-man playoff to capture his maiden PGA Tour victory at The RSM Classic on Sea Island, Georgia.

Sunday weather delays pushed completion of the tournament onto Monday for the second straight week on tour, setting up a tense dash around the coastal Seaside Course. Penge surged up the leaderboard down the stretch to post 16-under-par 266, ending regulation tied alongside China Haotong Li, American Michael Thorbjornsen, and Max Greyserman.

Returning to play sudden death, Penge delivered when it mattered most, winning on the very first playoff hole to seal his breakthrough triumph. Alongside the crystal trophy and a $1,332,000 winner check, the victory locks in full PGA Tour playing status for the English star while wrapping up the stressful autumn battle for 2027 tour cards. Next on the golf calendar is Tiger Woods 20-player Hero World Challenge in Albany, The Bahamas from 3 to 6 December.
