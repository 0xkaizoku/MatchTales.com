Taylor Fritz is in the Bybit Stockholm Open final today, Saturday 14 November, at Kungliga Tennishallen. The top seed advanced when Holger Rune withdrew from Thursday's quarterfinal (walkover) and then beat Lorenzo Musetti 6-4, 6-2 in 63 minutes on Friday. Thiago Agustin Tirante, ranked 51, reached the first ATP Tour final of his career by defeating Alejandro Tabilo 7-5, 7-6 (1). The championship match is upcoming. Do not publish a final score.

The Nitto ATP Finals in Turin still open Sunday 15 November at Inalpi Arena. Groups are unchanged from Thursday's draw: McEnroe (Zverev, Shelton, Fils, Jodar) and Lendl (Alcaraz, Medvedev, Cobolli, Tiafoe). Sunday singles: Zverev vs Jodar not before 2:00 p.m. CET, Shelton vs Fils not before 8:30 p.m. Monday: Alcaraz vs Tiafoe, Medvedev vs Cobolli. No Turin match has been played.
