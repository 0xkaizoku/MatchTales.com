San Francisco. The chilly breeze of the bay area was no match for the heated action on the pitch at Levi's Stadium. Japan showed once again that their technical precision is a force to be reckoned with. With a clinical 2-0 victory over Costa Rica, the Samurai Blue proved that they are ready for the knockout stages.

From the start, Takefusa Kubo and Kaoru Mitoma ran the wings with electric energy. The Costa Rican defense, anchored by the legendary Keylor Navas, fought hard to keep the game level. Navas made a string of spectacular saves early on, showing his timeless class under pressure.

However, the deadlock was broken before the break. Wataru Endo won a crucial duel in midfield and released Kubo, who found the back of the net with a precise low shot. In the second half, Japan doubled their lead when Mitoma cut inside and curled a beautiful effort past Navas. Costa Rica tried to hit back with Manfred Ugalde, but Japan's defense stood tall to secure a comfortable clean sheet.
