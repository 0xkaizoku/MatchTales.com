With just two days remaining before the illuminated streets of the Las Vegas Strip come alive with the sound of 1,000-horsepower hybrid engines, the Formula 1 paddock concluded its setup on Tuesday, 17 November, ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix (19-21 November).

While Mercedes teenage sensation Kimi Antonelli wrapped up the 2026 Drivers' World Championship in Brazil with an unassailable 390 points, the remaining three rounds offer fierce competition throughout the standings. Five drivers enter Las Vegas separated by just 33 points in the scrap for championship runner-up: Red Bull's Max Verstappen sits second on 279 points, closely shadowed by George Russell (262), Lewis Hamilton (261), Charles Leclerc (249), and McLaren's Lando Norris (246).

The 6.201-kilometer street track, featuring the iconic 1.9-kilometer full-throttle blast down Las Vegas Boulevard, presents unique technical demands. Ambient desert temperatures are forecast to dip below 12 degrees Celsius during night sessions, placing extreme demands on tire warmup and brake management. Teams must navigate heavy downforce trade-offs between high-speed straight-line velocity and low-speed traction through the complex hotel hairpins.

In the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes has already clinched its ninth title with 652 points. However, the fight for second place remains wide open, with Ferrari (510 points) holding a 95-point advantage over Red Bull (415), while McLaren (398) remains within striking distance ahead of Thursday night's opening practice sessions.
