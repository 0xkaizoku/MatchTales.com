East Rutherford. The grand stage of MetLife Stadium was the setting for a crucial clash as Germany took on Iraq. Germany showed signs of their clinical best, securing a solid 2-0 win to get their campaign back on track.

Iraq defended with immense pride and organization, cheered on by a loud and passionate set of fans. Zidane Iqbal showed flashes of brilliance in the midfield, but Germany's high press proved too much for the Asian side to build consistent attacks.

Jamal Musiala opened the scoring with a brilliant individual run, weaving through the defense before slotting it home. Florian Wirtz then doubled the lead with a clever volley after a cross from Kai Havertz. Iraq never gave up, but Germany controlled the game to secure the victory.
