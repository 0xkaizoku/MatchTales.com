## Why this game matters

Thursday gave the football world a 41-31 track meet in Buffalo. Sunday Night Football at Arrowhead Stadium answered with pure prime-time drama: a 30-30 deadlock across four quarters, followed by sudden-death overtime under the lights.

## What actually happened

Sky Sports, CBS and Chiefs.com detail a back-and-forth contest between two potent offenses. Patrick Mahomes orchestrated scoring drives late in the fourth quarter to keep Kansas City level, while Indianapolis countered with clinical execution to force a 30-30 finish at the end of regulation.

In overtime, the Kansas City defense produced a crucial stand on the opening possession. Handed the ball near midfield, Mahomes guided the offense into field-goal territory. Harrison Butker stepped up and drilled a clean 40-yard kick through the uprights to end the contest.

## What it changes

Kansas City improves to 2-0 atop the AFC West, demonstrating their trademark resilience in tight situations. Indianapolis leaves Arrowhead 0-2 despite a spirited showing on the road. Sunday night is officially closed with a 33-30 final in the books.
