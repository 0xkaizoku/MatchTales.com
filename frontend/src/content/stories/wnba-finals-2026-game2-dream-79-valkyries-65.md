## Game 2 is final

The Atlanta Dream beat the Golden State Valkyries 79-65 on Monday at Chase Center. Quarters: Dream 18, 21, 21, 19 and Valkyries 21, 16, 9, 19. The official scorer's report and ESPN agree. The series is tied 1-1. Game 1 still stands as Valkyries 68, Dream 64.

Angel Reese had 30 points and 16 rebounds, on 10 of 16 from the field and 9 of 11 at the free-throw line. Allisha Gray scored 18. Jordin Canada had 13 points and 10 assists. Gabby Williams scored 14 for Golden State. The official report lists attendance at 18,064, a sellout, and 9 Golden State points in the third quarter.

## Wednesday, still unplayed

Game 3 is Wednesday, 21 October, at 8:00 p.m. ET at State Farm Arena in Atlanta. ESPN, CBS and the league schedule put it on USA Network, Peacock and CNBC. No score is posted. ESPN lists Atlanta as a 3.5-point favorite. The winner on Wednesday will lead the Finals 2-1.
