## Why this game matters

If you want basketball with a scoreboard in September 2026, you want the WNBA. Minnesota had the one-seed. New York walked into Target Center on 18 September and printed 93-81. The playoffs still begin 27 September.

## What actually happened

AP: Miles a three-point play for 69-67 with 8:18 left. Stewart a 3 on the next possession. New York 23-12 the rest of the way. Stewart passed Sue Bird for ninth on the WNBA career scoring list at 6,806 points. Lynx 31-10, still the one-seed, three straight losses for the first time this season. Liberty 25-16.
