## Brennan rises on Championship Sunday

Michael Brennan emerged victorious from a dramatic Championship Sunday at Yokohama Country Club, firing a composed 4-under-par 67 to capture the inaugural Baycurrent Classic by a single stroke. Brennan finished at 16 under par (268), securing his third career PGA Tour title, a winner's paycheck of $1.44 million, and a guaranteed exemption through the 2028 season.

The tournament turned on the back nine of the par-71 West Course. 54-hole leader Max Homa struggled off the tee, stumbling to a closing 2-over 73 to finish tied for 11th at 10 under par. That opened the door for playing partners Jacob Bridgeman and Keith Mitchell. Bridgeman matched Brennan birdie for birdie before a costly bogey on the par-4 16th dropped him into a tie for second at 15 under with Mitchell, who shot a final-round 68.

## Matsuyama delights the home galleries

Local favorite Hideki Matsuyama electrified tens of thousands of Japanese fans with three consecutive birdies on the front nine, eventually settling for a share of sixth place at 11 under alongside Justin Thomas. Brennan's triumph marks a landmark moment for the PGA Tour Fall series, rewarding his laser iron play and stone-cold putting under relentless pressure in Yokohama.
