The final official tournament of the 2026 PGA Tour season got underway on Thursday morning, 19 November, as a 156-player field took to the Seaside and Plantation courses at Sea Island Golf Club for Round 1 of The RSM Classic (19-22 November).

Hosted by World Golf Hall of Famer Davis Love III on St. Simons Island, Georgia, the event marks the climax of the FedExCup Fall schedule. For players positioned near the number 125 in the standings, every stroke across the windswept coastal layouts carries life-altering professional consequences. Finishing inside the top 125 locks in full playing privileges for 2027, while those falling outside face conditional status or the gauntlet of qualifying school.

Local resident Davis Thompson wasted no time delighting the home galleries, grabbing the early clubhouse advantage on the par-70 Seaside Course by reaching 5-under par through 14 holes with six birdies. Two-time champion Mackenzie Hughes and recent Bermuda winner Austin Smotherman also opened brightly at 4-under par, while bubble contenders like Joel Dahmen and Zac Blair navigate every hole with razor-thin margins.

Simultaneously, players ranked 51st through 60th are contesting the Aon Next 10 exemptions for early-season Signature Events including Pebble Beach and Genesis. The high-stakes atmosphere at Sea Island ensures a riveting weekend of drama along the Georgia coast.
