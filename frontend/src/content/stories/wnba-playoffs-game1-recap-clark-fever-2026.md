## Why this one matters

The opening round of the 2026 WNBA postseason arrived with maximum intensity on Sunday, delivering a quadruple-header that immediately established the stakes of the best-of-three format. With history showing that Game 1 winners advance more than 75% of the time, the Indiana Fever's 86-80 road triumph over the Atlanta Dream shifted the entire bracket's dynamic.

Caitlin Clark showcased why she remains the league's most electrifying playmaker in her playoff performance. Dictating tempo from the opening tip and hitting clutch shots in crunch time, Clark helped Indiana steal home-court advantage and put Atlanta into an elimination corner heading into Game 2.

## What actually happened

WNBA.com and ESPN box scores detail four compelling Game 1 outcomes across the league:

In College Park, Georgia, the fifth-seeded Fever withstood a furious fourth-quarter rally from the fourth-seeded Dream. Clark finished with 24 points, 11 assists, and 6 rebounds, connecting on a signature 28-foot pull-up three with 1:14 remaining to put the game out of reach. Aliyah Boston anchored the paint with 18 points and 12 boards.

In Minneapolis, the No. 1 overall seed Minnesota Lynx put on a defensive clinic against the Washington Mystics, cruising to an 84-69 victory behind Napheesa Collier's 26 points and 9 rebounds.

At Chase Center in San Francisco, the New York Liberty fended off the Golden State Valkyries 79-71, relying on Breanna Stewart's 22 points and Courtney Vandersloot's steady floor leadership. In Las Vegas, the two-time defending champion Aces defeated the Dallas Wings 92-81 as A'ja Wilson dominated with 28 points, 14 rebounds, and 4 blocks.

## The numbers that tell the story

- **Fever road win:** Indiana 86, Atlanta 80 (Fever lead series 1-0).
- **Clark statline:** 24 points, 11 assists, 4 three-pointers made.
- **Lynx defense:** Minnesota held Washington to 37.1% shooting from the floor in an 84-69 win.
- **Wilson dominance:** 28 points, 14 rebounds, 4 blocks in Aces' 92-81 victory over Wings.
- **Format pressure:** In best-of-three series, trailing teams face elimination in Game 2 starting Tuesday.
