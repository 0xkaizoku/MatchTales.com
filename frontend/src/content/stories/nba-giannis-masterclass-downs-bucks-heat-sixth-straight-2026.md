Giannis Antetokounmpo delivered an emphatic emotional statement on Wednesday night, 18 November, torturing his former franchise with 27 points, 14 rebounds, and nine assists to guide the Miami Heat to a breathtaking 137-130 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks at Kaseya Center.

In his first meeting against Milwaukee since his landmark trade, Giannis dictated the rhythm from the opening tip, punishing single coverage in the paint and creating open corner looks for Miami's perimeter shooters. Frontcourt partner Bam Adebayo delivered 26 points and 10 rebounds, while Tyler Herro contributed 23 points, helping Miami shoot 54.3 percent from the floor as a team.

Milwaukee refused to go quietly behind Kyle Kuzma (26 points) and Damian Lillard (24 points, 8 assists), but a pivotal 14-4 Heat surge in the opening minutes of the fourth quarter created a double-digit cushion that Miami preserved down the stretch. The victory extends Miami's winning streak to six games and lifts the Heat to 9-3 on the season.

Elsewhere on Wednesday, Nikola Jokic notched 24 points, nine assists, and eight rebounds as the Denver Nuggets erased a 17-point first-half deficit to upset the Boston Celtics 105-101 at Ball Arena, handing Boston its second consecutive loss. In Toronto, Anthony Davis powered the Washington Wizards past the Raptors 103-99 with a 25-point, 14-rebound double-double.
