The Premier League roared back to life on Saturday, 21 November, following the conclusion of the international break, and drama unfolded immediately at the Etihad Stadium in the lunchtime kickoff.

Fulham stunned reigning champions Manchester City by seizing a 1-0 lead in the 16th minute, as Brazilian forward Rodrigo Muniz rose unmarked to power a clinical header past Ederson from an Antonee Robinson cross. Pep Guardiola team dominated possession, yet Marco Silva well-drilled Londoners threatened on the counter-attack, putting City under immense early pressure in their pursuit of top spot.

The stakes could hardly be higher across Matchweek 11. Table-topping Arsenal, currently leading the league on 27 points, travel north to face Newcastle United at St. James Park in the late 17:30 GMT kickoff. With surprise challengers Brighton sitting second on 22 points and Manchester City frozen on 21 points pending today outcome, any slip at the Etihad could widen Arsenal path toward winter dominance.
