## Green Bay rolls in freezing Wisconsin elements

The Green Bay Packers opened Week 8 of the 2026 NFL season with an authoritative 27-17 victory over the Carolina Panthers on Thursday night, 29 October, in front of 77,844 frost-bitten supporters at Lambeau Field. The triumph improves Matt LaFleur's team to 6-2 on the season, keeping them firmly in the rear-view mirror of the 6-0 Minnesota Vikings in a fiercely contested NFC North divisional race.

Quarterback Jordan Love continued his stellar autumn campaign, delivering a poised 20-of-28 performance for 245 passing yards, two touchdowns, and zero turnovers. Love connected with wideout Jayden Reed on a pinpoint 18-yard corner strike in the second quarter, before finding tight end Luke Musgrave for a 12-yard score early in the third. Running back Josh Jacobs served as the offensive battering ram, racking up 94 rushing yards on 19 carries and plunging over the goal line from two yards out to establish an early 10-0 advantage.

## Gary leads ferocious defensive suffocation of Young

While Green Bay's offense operated with clinical precision, defensive coordinator Jeff Hafley's unit delivered the decisive blow against Carolina's pocket. Edge rusher Rashan Gary tormented young Panthers quarterback Bryce Young throughout the night, recording two sacks, three quarterback hits, and forcing a critical hurried throw that resulted in a third-quarter interception by safety Xavier McKinney.

Young fought valiantly in the cold conditions, completing 18 of 32 passes for 198 yards and a fourth-quarter touchdown to rookie receiver Xavier Legette, but Carolina could not sustain drives against Green Bay's front seven. The Panthers drop to 3-5, while Green Bay now enjoys a mini-bye before preparing for their Week 9 divisional showdown against Detroit. With Minnesota defending their perfect 6-0 record on Sunday, the NFC playoff picture grows more intense by the week.
