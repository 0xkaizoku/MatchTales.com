Associated Press posted complete second-round scores Friday, 13 November, from the Earth Course at Jumeirah Golf Estates. Rory McIlroy shot 69-70 for 139 (five under) and shares the DP World Tour Championship lead with Tyrrell Hatton (70-69). Joost Luiten, who opened with 67, added a 73 for 140 alongside Kristoffer Reitan (70-70). Mikael Lindberg, Victor Perez and Freddy Schott sit at 141.

Race to Dubai leader Eugenio Lopez-Chacarra is 72-71 for 143, tied for 11th with Francesco Laporta, Adrian Meronk and first-round runner-up Jacob Skov Olesen (68-75). The $10 million season finale has 36 holes remaining. Round 3 is in progress Saturday 14 November and is not published as a final. Sunday is the fourth round.
