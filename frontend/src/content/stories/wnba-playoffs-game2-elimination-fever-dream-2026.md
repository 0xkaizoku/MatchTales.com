## Why this one matters

The 2026 WNBA postseason moves at breakneck speed, and by Tuesday midnight, two franchises could already be celebrating semifinal berths. Headlining the evening slate is Game 2 between the Indiana Fever and Atlanta Dream at Gateway Center Arena, where the hosts face sudden-death elimination.

Indiana holds all the cards following Sunday's 86-80 road victory. In her playoff debut, Caitlin Clark delivered a masterclass in game management, dishing 11 assists, scoring 24 points, and burying two decisive step-back three-pointers in the closing two minutes. Alongside center Aliyah Boston, who dominated the paint with 18 points and 12 rebounds, Indiana proved their regular-season surge was built for playoff basketball.

## What Atlanta must change

For the Atlanta Dream, Tuesday represents their entire season in 40 minutes. Head coach Tanisha Wright must find answers to Clark's pick-and-roll orchestration without leaving Boston and Kelsey Mitchell unattended. Allisha Gray and Rhyne Howard combined for 44 points in Game 1, but Atlanta surrendered 14 second-chance points on the offensive glass.

If Atlanta can increase defensive ball pressure and control defensive rebounds, they can push the series back to Indianapolis for a deciding Game 3. If they hesitate, Clark and the Fever will celebrate their first semifinal appearance in a decade.

## Across the Tuesday bracket

- **Fever at Dream (7:30 p.m. ET):** Indiana leads 1-0; a Fever victory clinches a semifinal berth.
- **Mystics at Lynx (8:00 p.m. ET):** Top-seeded Minnesota looks to finish off Washington at Target Center following Napheesa Collier's 26-point Game 1.
- **Format stakes:** In the best-of-three opening round, there is zero cushion for error.
- **Wednesday ahead:** Game 2 for New York vs Golden State and Las Vegas vs Dallas takes center stage tomorrow.
