## Why Sunday at Anfield was the defining test

Liverpool against Manchester City at Anfield was billed as the defining fixture of early autumn, and for an hour it lived up to every syllable of the hype. Both heavyweights traded tactical blows in a tense, scoreless first half where Arne Slot's defensive shape stifled City's customary passing lanes. But the match tilted irrevocably in the 62nd minute when Ryan Gravenberch was shown a second yellow card for a late challenge on Phil Foden.

Reduced to ten men, Liverpool could not withstand City's relentless numerical superiority. In the 65th minute, Erling Haaland broke the deadlock, rising above Virgil van Dijk to power home Kevin De Bruyne's curling cross for his ninth league goal of the campaign. Three minutes later, Jeremy Doku cut inside from the left flank and arrowed an unstoppable 20-yard finish into Alisson's top corner.

## The wider title race implications

Rayan Cherki added a third in the 78th minute off a swift counterattack, sealing an emphatic 3-0 triumph that silences Anfield and sends City three points clear atop the Premier League on 18 points (6-0-0).

Arsenal sit second on 15 points following their Saturday win over Leeds, while Liverpool suffer their first league defeat of the Slot era, slipping to seventh on 9 points. In a title race with no margin for error, Pep Guardiola's squad showed why their relentless standard remains the benchmark of modern football.
