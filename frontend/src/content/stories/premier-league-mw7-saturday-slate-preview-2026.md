## Thursday briefings before the weekend return

Premier League managers take the podium on Thursday to provide team news and tactical insights ahead of Matchweek 7, which begins Saturday 17 October across England.

Manchester City occupy the top of the table on a flawless 18 points from six matches (6-0-0). Pep Guardiola's side have scored 16 goals while conceding only 5, punctuated by their emphatic 3-0 win against Liverpool at Anfield before the break. Erling Haaland continues to set the pace atop the scoring charts.

Mikel Arteta's Arsenal remain the closest pursuers in second place on 15 points (5-0-1), following their 2-1 victory over Leeds United. Brighton and Hove Albion sit third on 13 points (4-1-1) after their resilient start to the campaign.

## The state of the table

Matchweek 6 completed on Monday evening when Coventry City produced an extraordinary 4-1 victory over Newcastle United at the Coventry Building Society Arena. Jack Rudoni opened the scoring with a sixth-minute penalty, followed by goals from Sidiki Cherif, Sven Botman, Ellis Simms, and Stephen Mfuni.

The result propelled Frank Lampard's Coventry side to 14th on 6 points, level with Manchester United. Newcastle remained 11th on 8 points.

At the lower end of the table, Tottenham Hotspur reside in 19th position on 3 points, eager for a turnaround when league play resumes on Saturday. This desk records only completed results and does not predict future scores.
