## Fall hiatus before island golf

The PGA Tour has no official tournament round scheduled for Saturday 17 October. Players and organizers are in transition following the completion of the Baycurrent Classic in Yokohama, Japan, where Michael Brennan earned his maiden PGA Tour title at 16 under par with a four-round total of 268.

Brennan's two-shot victory over Keith Mitchell and Jacob Bridgeman remains the latest entry in the 2026 FedExCup Fall standings.

## Port Royal prepares for Bermuda Championship

Competitive action resumes next week with the Butterfield Bermuda Championship, scheduled for 22-25 October at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda. The event offers a 6 million dollar purse, 500 FedExCup points to the winner, and a critical opportunity for players aiming to secure top-125 status for full 2027 tour cards.

Adam Schenk is the defending tournament champion, having posted a final-round 71 amid challenging coastal winds to prevail by a single stroke.
