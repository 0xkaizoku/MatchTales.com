Tyrrell Hatton captured his ninth DP World Tour title and sixth Rolex Series crown on Sunday, 15 November, firing a brilliant bogey-free 66 (-6) on the Earth Course at Jumeirah Golf Estates to finish 15 under par (273) and claim a dominant five-shot victory at the DP World Tour Championship. Starting the day with a one-shot lead, Hatton birdied four of his first eight holes and never looked back, putting on a masterclass in Dubai.

The season-long Race to Dubai delivered pure theater down to the 72nd green. Spaniard Eugenio Chacarra, who held the live standings lead on the back nine, shot a final-round 68 to finish tied for third at 9 under (279) alongside Oliver Lindell. But Rory McIlroy produced champion poise, carding a bogey-free 67 (-5) highlighted by a decisive birdie on the par-4 16th to claim solo second at 10 under par (278).

McIlroy's runner-up finish secured his eighth European Tour Order of Merit title and fifth consecutive Harry Vardon Trophy by just 125 points, equalling Colin Montgomerie's historic record. In an emotional greenside interview, McIlroy dedicated the triumph to his father Gerry, who spent weeks in a Cleveland ICU recovering from a double-lung transplant earlier this autumn.
