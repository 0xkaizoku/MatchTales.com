## The Showdown at Hard Rock Stadium

On 2026-07-18, the footballing world turns its attention to Hard Rock Stadium in Miami for **France** vs **England** in the Third-Place Play-Off.

The last four are decided. Spain and Argentina wait in the final, and the other half of the semi-final story now turns to Miami. France and England still have a medal on the table, even if neither squad dreamed of this fixture a week ago.

Rest-day work is tactical and emotional. Managers balance rotation against pride. Players on the fringe of club futures still see a stage that can change a career. Supporters who followed every knockout night want one more performance that feels like them.

Kick-off is 18 July at Hard Rock Stadium. Bronze is not the trophy, but it is still a podium finish on the biggest stage in sport.
