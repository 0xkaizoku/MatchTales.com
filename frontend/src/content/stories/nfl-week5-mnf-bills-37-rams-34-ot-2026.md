## Overtime at SoFi

The Buffalo Bills beat the Los Angeles Rams 37-34 in overtime at SoFi Stadium. ESPN's quarters: Bills 6, 7, 6, 15 and 3. Rams 0, 7, 7, 20 and 0. Buffalo are 4-1 and 2-0 on the road. Los Angeles are 2-3 and 1-2 at home, still behind the 5-0 49ers in the NFC West.

Tyler Bass won it with a 45-yard field goal with 1:55 left in overtime. Greg Rousseau had blocked a Harrison Mevis field-goal attempt. James Cook fumbled early in overtime, and that Bills drive ended. ESPN's recap counted six lead changes.

## The lines that decided it

Josh Allen passed for 210 yards and rushed for 121, which is 331 total yards and two touchdowns. The Bills were without receivers DJ Moore and Joshua Palmer, and Allen completed only four passes to receivers. James Cook rushed for 111 yards and two touchdowns. Matthew Stafford passed for 383 yards and three touchdowns. Puka Nacua had 17 receptions for 138 yards and a score.

The rest of the Week 5 board is unchanged. The Vikings and the 49ers are 5-0. The Chiefs are 4-0 on their bye. The Ravens are 4-1 after a 30-23 overtime win over the Falcons. Thursday's score remains Buccaneers 24, Cowboys 16.
