Tyrrell Hatton carded a four-under 68 on Saturday, 14 November, to seize the solo 54-hole lead at nine-under 207 in the DP World Tour Championship at the Earth Course, Jumeirah Golf Estates in Dubai. France's Victor Perez fired a five-under 67 to sit alone in second at eight-under 208, with Sweden's Mikael Lindberg third on 210 after a 69.

The high-stakes battle for the season-long Race to Dubai crown drew level on Saturday afternoon. Race leader Eugenio Chacarra produced a clinical four-under 68 to reach five-under 211, erasing a four-shot overnight deficit to join Rory McIlroy, who struggled around the turn with three bogeys in a level-par 72. Both share fourth at 211 (-5), leaving Chacarra in projected position to secure the Harry Vardon Trophy entering Sunday's final round. Jon Rahm is three under (71-71-71=213).
