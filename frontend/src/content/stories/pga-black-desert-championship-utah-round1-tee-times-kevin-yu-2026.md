## Why this tournament matters

The last time a PGA Tour champion raised a trophy in the state of Utah, John F. Kennedy was in the White House, gasoline was thirty cents a gallon, and Tommy Jacobs won the 1963 Utah Open.

On Thursday morning in Ivins, the PGA Tour ends that 61-year absence in the most breathtaking natural amphitheater imaginable. Carved into ancient black basalt lava flows that contrast vividly against the red Navajo sandstone walls of Snow Canyon, the Black Desert Resort Golf Course represents the final completed design of major champion Tom Weiskopf. It is a par-71 visual masterpiece where jagged volcanic rock punishes errant tee shots and high desert winds make club selection treacherous.

## What is happening on the course

Thursday's marquee pairing is pure poetic theatre. Tournament organizers have grouped newly minted Sanderson Farms champion Kevin Yu alongside Keith Mitchell and Beau Hossler.

Just four days ago in Jackson, Mississippi, Mitchell held a commanding three-shot lead entering Sunday before a closing 70 opened the door for Yu and Hossler to force extra holes. Yu converted a birdie on the first playoff hole to capture his first PGA Tour title in his 57th start.

The victory had monumental professional ramifications: Yu vaulted 36 spots in the FedExCup Fall standings up to No. 60, putting him right in the bubble for the Aon Next 10. For Hossler and Mitchell, Thursday morning at 07:45 MDT is an immediate opportunity to turn Sunday's heartbreak into redemption.

## What it changes for the FedExCup Fall

With only six events remaining in the 2026 autumn calendar, the mathematics of survival are unforgiving.

Players ranked 51st through 60th at the conclusion of the RSM Classic will punch tickets to two $20 million Signature Events in 2027: the AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am and The Genesis Invitational. Meanwhile, everyone hovering outside the top 125 is grinding for their PGA Tour cards and livelihood. Across these black volcanic corridors, every single par putt has career-altering consequences.
