## Why this note matters

MatchTales was built to answer one question about one tournament: why should you care about this World Cup match? Spain answered the tournament on 19 July. Ferran Torres, 106th minute, 1-0 after extra time. The question did not expire.

## What we cover now

Soccer, including the World Cup archive and the Premier League that started in August. Tennis, Wimbledon through a US Open that is still live. Golf, Fox at Birkdale, Scheffler at East Lake. Football, Super Bowl LX as the reason Wednesday's kickoff is a rematch. Basketball, Knicks in June, WNBA in September. Formula 1, Silverstone through Monza, Madrid next.

## How we behave

If a scorer, a minute or a ranking is on the page, it is locked to a named source. If two sources disagree, we print the score they share and we skip the rest. We do not invent quotes. We do not use em-dashes. We do not pretend a Tuesday in September is still 19 July.

The World Cup is a finished novel on the shelf. The desk is open.
