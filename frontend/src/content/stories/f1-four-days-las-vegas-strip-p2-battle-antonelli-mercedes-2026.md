The Formula 1 paddock is just four days away from track action under the dazzling neon lights of Nevada for Round 21, the Las Vegas Grand Prix (19-21 November). Cars will roar down the iconic 6.201 km Strip circuit for Thursday night practice, building toward a high-stakes Saturday night main race on 21 November. No competitive sessions have yet taken place for this event.

While 20-year-old sensation Kimi Antonelli (390 points) and Mercedes-AMG (652 points) sealed both the Drivers and Constructors World Championships in Brazil, the scrap for second place in the standings is unprecedentedly fierce. Only 33 points separate second-placed Max Verstappen (279) from fifth-placed Lando Norris (246), with George Russell (262), Lewis Hamilton (261), and Charles Leclerc (249) sandwiched tightly between them.

With three rounds remaining in Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi, constructor pride and tens of millions in prize money hang in the balance. Ferrari (510 points) hold second over Red Bull Racing (415) and McLaren (398), with cold desert night temperatures and low-grip asphalt expected to test tire warm-up strategies to the limit.
