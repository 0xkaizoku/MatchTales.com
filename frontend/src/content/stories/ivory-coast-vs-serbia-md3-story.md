Vancouver. The turf at BC Place hosted a bruising encounter between the champions of Africa and the physical giants of the Balkans. Ivory Coast edged Serbia 2-1 in a match that was filled with high drama and intense passion.

Serbia went ahead early through a powerful strike from Dusan Vlahovic, who latched onto a pass from strike partner Aleksandar Mitrovic. The Serbian physical approach dominated the early exchanges, but the Elephants slowly clawed their way back into the contest.

Simon Adingra was the catalyst for the comeback, terrorizing the Serbian fullbacks with his pace. Sebastien Haller equalized with a towering header, and Franck Kessie scored the winner from the penalty spot after a handball in the box. Serbia pushed hard for an equalizer, but Ivory Coast held on for a historic win.
