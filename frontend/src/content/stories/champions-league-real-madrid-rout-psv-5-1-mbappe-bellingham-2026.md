Real Madrid affirmed their standing atop European football on Tuesday night, 24 November, turning a tense Champions League Matchday 5 encounter into a 5-1 second-half showcase at the Santiago Bernabeu to become the sole side with a maximum 15 points in the 36-team league phase.

PSV Eindhoven stunned Madrid before the interval when veteran winger Ivan Perisic converted cleanly from the penalty spot in the 32nd minute. Carlo Ancelotti side needed only five minutes after the restart to ignite, however, as Federico Valverde slammed home the 50th-minute equalizer before Jude Bellingham unleashed a curling wonder-goal from outside the box in the 60th minute to swing the contest.

Once ahead, Real Madrid overwhelmed the Dutch champions across a ruthless final half-hour. Kylian Mbappe struck twice in the 64th and 84th minutes before Vinicius Junior completed the scoring on 87 minutes. Elsewhere across Tuesday European action, Viktor Gyokeres thundered Arsenal 400th Champions League goal in the 10th minute before Maximilian Beier secured a 1-1 draw for Borussia Dortmund, and Manchester City drew 2-2 with Napoli ahead of Wednesday 20:00 UTC ties. In the Premier League entering Matchweek 12, Arsenal remain top on 28 points after drawing Newcastle 2-2, six ahead of Brighton and Man City (22 apiece).
