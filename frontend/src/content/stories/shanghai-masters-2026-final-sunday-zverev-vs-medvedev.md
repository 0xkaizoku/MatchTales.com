## The championship match was Spanish

The ATP results page lists Sunday's final on Stadium Court as finished in 2 hours 27 minutes. Alejandro Davidovich Fokina beat Rafael Jodar 1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4. A preview that sent Alexander Zverev against Daniil Medvedev did not survive Saturday.

Davidovich Fokina beat Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 in the first semifinal. Jodar beat Medvedev 6-3, 6-4 in the second. Jodar, 20, was playing his first Masters 1000 final and was the youngest Shanghai finalist in tournament history.

Zverev remains the year-end world No. 1 after the French Open and the US Open, with Jannik Sinner out for the season. He said after the semifinal that he needed a break before Vienna, which starts on 24 October. The trophy is Davidovich Fokina's.
