## Spurs conquer Stamford Bridge after eight years

Premier League Matchweek 8 delivered seismic shocks across the table on Saturday, 24 October, headlined by Tottenham Hotspur securing their first away league victory at Stamford Bridge since April 2018 with a dramatic 2-0 triumph over Chelsea. The London derby remained deadlocked until the 77th minute, when a dangerous corner delivery resulted in Chelsea goalkeeper Mike Penders deflecting the ball into his own net under heavy pressure.

Chelsea's evening deteriorated further three minutes later when Cole Palmer received a second yellow card for dissent. Down to ten men, the Blues pushed forward in search of an equalizer but were caught on the break in the 87th minute as Mathys Tel finished off a blistering solo run to seal the points for Tottenham. The victory lifted Spurs to 9 points in 12th place, while Chelsea remained frozen in fourth on 13 points.

## Villa halt City streak as Arsenal go level top

The tremors began earlier in the afternoon at Villa Park, where Aston Villa produced an inspired performance to defeat leaders Manchester City 2-1. Nicolas Jackson struck in the 6th and 60th minutes, sandwiching Erling Haaland's 52nd-minute equalizer, to snap City's unblemished seven-match winning streak. It marked City's first league defeat of the season and left Pep Guardiola's squad on 21 points (7-0-1).

Arsenal took full advantage in North London, cruising past Everton 2-0 with goals from Ezri Konsa in the 7th minute and Gabriel Magalhaes in the 68th minute. The Gunners pulled level with Manchester City at the top of the Premier League table on 21 points, setting up a thrilling two-horse race as the autumn title race intensifies.
