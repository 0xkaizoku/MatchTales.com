## Why this match matters

International football takes center stage across Europe on Wednesday as squads put the final touches on preparations for Thursday's ten-match UEFA Nations League schedule. The continental spotlight falls squarely on Group A2, where Luciano Spalletti's resurgent Italy host Domenico Tedesco's Belgium at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome. The Azzurri took control of the group during September with confident victories over France and Israel, but face a stern physical examination against a Belgian side battling to stay alive in the race for a top-two quarterfinal seed.

## What actually happened

Elsewhere, England prepare for a critical Wembley date against Greece on Thursday night. Lee Carsley's side produced fireworks in September with a 7-0 dismantling of Croatia in Rijeka and expect to dominate possession against Ivan Jovanovic's organized Greeks. Meanwhile, Portugal already sit comfortably in next spring's quarterfinals after Roberto Martinez's squad notched their fourth straight victory with a 2-1 win over Norway in Porto behind strikes from Joao Cancelo and Goncalo Ramos.

## What happens next

Club competitions remain paused during the window, with Manchester City leading the frozen Premier League on a perfect 15 points from five matches. With domestic leagues set to restart on Saturday 10 October, this mid-week international slate offers managers their final opportunity to calibrate tactics ahead of a demanding autumn marathon.
