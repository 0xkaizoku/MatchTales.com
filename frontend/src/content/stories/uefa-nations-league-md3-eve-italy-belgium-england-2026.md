## What this note replaces

This page replaces a preview that said Italy would host Belgium in Rome and England would host Greece at Wembley on this window. Those were not the matches.

## What was played

On Monday 5 October, France beat Belgium 4-1 and Italy beat Turkiye 3-1 in Bologna. On Tuesday 6 October, England beat Czechia 3-0 and Spain beat Croatia 2-1 away. Spain and Portugal are through on 12 points. The full table is on the 8 October Nations League story.
