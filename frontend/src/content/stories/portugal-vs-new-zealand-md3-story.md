Los Angeles. The lights of Hollywood shone brightly on the SoFi Stadium, but it was Cristiano Ronaldo who stole the spotlight. Portugal put on a dominant display to defeat New Zealand 3-0, confirming their place in the knockout rounds with style.

New Zealand, captained by Chris Wood, fought with incredible determination. They set up in a compact low block, challenging Portugal to break them down. Marko Stamenic put in a tireless shift in the kiwi midfield, trying to disrupt the Portuguese rhythm.

But the quality of A Selecao eventually shone through. Bruno Fernandes unlocked the defense with a sublime pass to Rafael Leao, who set up the first goal. Ronaldo then doubled the lead with a trademark header, sending the stadium into a frenzy. A late third goal sealed a comfortable night for Portugal, while New Zealand exited with their heads held high.
