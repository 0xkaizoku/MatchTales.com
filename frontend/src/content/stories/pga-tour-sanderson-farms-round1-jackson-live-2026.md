## Why this one matters

Following Wednesday's pro-am festivities, the serious business of tour survival began Thursday morning at the Country Club of Jackson. With 500 FedExCup points, a winner's check of 1.44 million dollars, and a life-changing two-year PGA Tour exemption on the line, the opening round of the Sanderson Farms Championship immediately delivered fireworks.

Taking full advantage of receptive bermudagrass greens and calm morning air, Chesson Hadley and Beau Hossler set the clubhouse benchmark with matching 6-under 66s. Hadley, seeking his first tour victory since 2014, strung together five consecutive birdies around the turn. Hossler joined him atop the leaderboard with a flawless bogey-free card that featured eagle on the par-five 11th hole.

## Kevin Yu launches title defense

All eyes in the morning wave followed defending champion Kevin Yu. The Chinese Taipei standout, who captured his breakthrough tour victory here in a playoff two years ago, proved that his affinity for Jackson remains intact.

Yu opened his title defense with a confident 4-under 68, carding four birdies and zero bogeys to sit comfortably inside the top five. Playing alongside him, two-time PGA Tour winner Mackenzie Hughes and Emiliano Grillo each signed for 3-under 69s. With afternoon winds freshening and Rickie Fowler among the late starters, the leaderboard promises tight compression heading into Friday's cut line battle.

## The numbers that tell the story

- **Early leaders:** Chesson Hadley and Beau Hossler share the clubhouse lead at 6-under (66).
- **Defending champion:** Kevin Yu sits two strokes back following an opening 4-under 68.
- **Top contenders:** Mackenzie Hughes (-3, 69) and Emiliano Grillo (-3, 69) remain in close contention.
- **Course setup:** Country Club of Jackson played at 7,461 yards with soft greens yielding low scores.
- **Stakes:** Top 125 players on the FedExCup Fall points list retain fully exempt status for the 2027 PGA Tour season.
