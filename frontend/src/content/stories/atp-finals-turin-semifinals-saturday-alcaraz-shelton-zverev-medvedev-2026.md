The stage is set for an electrifying Super Saturday at Inalpi Arena in Turin as the final four contenders battle for a place in Sunday championship match of the 2026 Nitto ATP Finals.

Semifinal 1 (14:00 CET) pits Ivan Lendl Group winner Carlos Alcaraz against American heavy-hitter Ben Shelton. Alcaraz was in majestic form across the round-robin stage, sweeping aside Daniil Medvedev (6-2, 6-4), Frances Tiafoe (6-3, 6-4), and Flavio Cobolli without dropping a single set to finish 3-0. Shelton claimed the runner-up spot in the John McEnroe Group with a 2-1 record, defeating Arthur Fils in three sets and Rafael Jodar 6-3, 6-4 on Thursday to reach his first year-end semifinal.

Semifinal 2 (20:30 CET) renews one of modern tennis most fierce rivalries: Alexander Zverev against Daniil Medvedev. World No. 1 Zverev topped the McEnroe Group with three straight-sets victories, including a clutch 6-4, 7-6 (5) triumph over Arthur Fils on Thursday night. Medvedev secured second place in the Lendl Group with clinical wins over Tiafoe and Cobolli. With 1,500 ranking points and the prestigious season-ending trophy within reach, Saturday in Piedmont promises world-class drama.
