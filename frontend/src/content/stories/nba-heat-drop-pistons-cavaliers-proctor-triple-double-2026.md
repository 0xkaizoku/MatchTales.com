The Miami Heat delivered an offensive masterpiece on Saturday night, 14 November, pulling away from the Detroit Pistons for a 137-117 victory at Kaseya Center. Giannis Antetokounmpo paced the Heat with 34 points, 14 rebounds, and 8 assists, shooting 13 of 19 from the field. Sharpshooter Klay Thompson returned after missing two games with stiffness, catching fire from the perimeter to hit 7 three-pointers on his way to 26 points. Cade Cunningham led Detroit with 33 points and 8 assists in a losing effort.

In Cleveland, an improbable masterclass unfolded at Rocket Arena as the Cavaliers defeated the Dallas Mavericks 127-117 despite resting four starters, including Donovan Mitchell, James Harden, and Evan Mobley. Guard Tyrese Proctor seized the spotlight, recording his first career triple-double with 24 points, 11 rebounds, and 10 assists, withstanding Kyrie Irving's 34 points and Cooper Flagg's 18 for Dallas.

Across the Western Conference, the Denver Nuggets held off the New Orleans Pelicans 116-108 behind Nikola Jokic's late heroics. Meanwhile, the San Antonio Spurs enjoyed an off day following Friday's 123-85 destruction of Golden State, having stretched their league-leading winning streak to 11 consecutive games behind Stephon Castle and Victor Wembanyama.
