## A scheduled hiatus before Port Royal

The PGA Tour has no tournament scheduled for the week of 14 October 2026. Players are taking a one-week break before the FedEx Cup Fall slate resumes with the Butterfield Bermuda Championship, scheduled for 22-25 October at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda.

Port Royal is a par-71 layout measuring 6,828 yards along the Atlantic coastline. The tournament offers a total purse of $6 million, with Adam Schenk listed as the defending champion.

## Yokohama result in the books

The victory by 24-year-old Michael Brennan at the Baycurrent Classic in Yokohama, Japan, stands as the most recent official result. Brennan closed with a 4-under 68 at Yokohama Country Club for a four-round total of 268 (16 under par), securing his breakthrough PGA Tour title and a first-place check of $1.44 million.

Keith Mitchell and Jacob Bridgeman finished tied for second at 15 under par. Max Homa tied for 13th at 11 under. As qualifying exemptions and sponsor invitations are finalized on Wednesday for Bermuda, the fall standings remain tightly contested for players seeking top-125 status for 2027.
