## Why this one matters

In the high-stakes theater of best-of-five playoff series, Game 2 is universally recognized as the pivotal swing encounter. Fall behind 0-2 on the road, and history suggests your season is finished: teams trailing 0-2 in WNBA postseason history have advanced in only 4 percent of matchups.

That harsh reality hung heavy in the air across practice facilities in Brooklyn and Minneapolis on Monday. The two-time defending champion Las Vegas Aces and the upstart Indiana Fever spent the day dissecting tape, confronting the tactical flaws that saw them drop Sunday's series openers to the New York Liberty and Minnesota Lynx respectively.

For Las Vegas, the challenge is reclaiming their trademark swagger against a Liberty squad that dominated the paint. For Indiana, it is finding a way to withstand Minnesota's disciplined half-court defense while unleashing Caitlin Clark in transition.

## Film room revelations

Monday's coaching clinics in New York and Minneapolis centered on specific tactical corrections:

- **Aces turnover crisis:** Las Vegas committed 18 costly turnovers in Game 1, conceding 22 fast-break points to New York. Head coach Becky Hammon emphasized ball security and sharper spacing to prevent Liberty defenders from swarming A'ja Wilson when the MVP posts up.
- **Stewart's defensive masterclass:** Breanna Stewart anchored New York with 34 points and eight blocked shots on Sunday. Hammon's counter-strategy requires getting Stewart moving laterally on perimeter switches to open driving lanes for Kelsey Plum and Jackie Young.
- **Containing Napheesa Collier:** Minnesota's Collier has been virtually unstoppable, putting up 28 points on 11-of-17 shooting in Game 1. Indiana coach Christie Sides worked on blitzing Collier on high screen-and-rolls, daring Minnesota's secondary shooters to beat them.
- **Clark's fourth-quarter blueprint:** After a slow start against Minnesota's physical traps, Caitlin Clark ignited Indiana with 14 fourth-quarter points, finishing with 22 points and nine assists. The Fever plan to implement earlier cross-screens to liberate Clark from Courtney Williams' full-court ball pressure.

## Tuesday night showdowns

The stage is set for a massive Tuesday evening across the WNBA. Either the Liberty and Lynx establish commanding 2-0 leads that put them on the brink of the Finals, or the Aces and Fever deliver the road counterpunches necessary to seize home-court advantage.
