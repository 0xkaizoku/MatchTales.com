## Why this one matters

Through six rounds of the 2026-27 Premier League campaign, one fact towers over the division: Pep Guardiola's Manchester City have not blinked. Six matches played, six victories secured, 18 points banked. As Matchweek 7 approaches, the question across English football is whether anyone can disrupt the champions before their lead becomes insurmountable.

Leading the charge is Erling Haaland, who has rewritten modern scoring benchmarks with 10 goals in six games. Following his two-goal performance in Saturday's 2-1 win over Newcastle, the Norwegian is averaging 1.67 goals per 90 minutes. With Kevin De Bruyne and Phil Foden creating chances at will, City travel to West London on Saturday to face Brentford with a seventh consecutive win firmly in their sights.

## The chasing pack under pressure

Behind City, the pressure is immense. Arsenal sit second on 15 points (5-0-1), having recovered from their Matchweek 5 defeat at Brighton with a composed 2-0 victory against Everton. Mikel Arteta knows any slip-up by City represents Arsenal's window to pull level on points, making their home clash with Southampton critical.

Liverpool sit third on 12 points following back-to-back victories under Arne Slot. Meanwhile, Brighton remain the surprise story of the autumn in fourth on 11 points, having scored a league-best 17 goals. For clubs harboring title ambitions, Matchweek 7 represents a weekend where dropped points could establish a four-point chasm behind Manchester City.

## Key fixtures to watch in Matchweek 7

- **Brentford vs Manchester City:** City look to maintain their 100% record against a stubborn Bees side that has troubled them in past campaigns.
- **Arsenal vs Southampton:** The Gunners aim to keep pace at Emirates Stadium behind Bukayo Saka and Martin Odegaard.
- **Crystal Palace vs Liverpool:** Slot's side visits Selhurst Park seeking their fifth league win.
- **The golden boot chase:** Haaland (10 goals) leads Bryan Mbeumo (5) and Cole Palmer (5) into October.
