Europe's premier international clash takes center stage Sunday night, 15 November, as European champions Spain welcome England to the Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid (19:45 GMT) to decide top spot in UEFA Nations League Group A3. Luis de la Fuente's Spain sit atop the section on 13 points, requiring only a draw on home soil to secure seeded status in the spring quarter-finals.

England and manager Thomas Tuchel travel to the Spanish capital knowing that only a victory can leapfrog the hosts following Thursday's hard-fought 2-1 victory over Croatia at Wembley, where Harry Kane converted a 64th-minute penalty on his record-extending 126th cap. The rematch of the Euro 2024 final promises intense tactical drama in front of 68,000 spectators in Madrid.

Saturday night's Nations League slate delivered a seismic shock in Lisbon, where Denmark dismantled Portugal 4-1 in League A Group 4 to throw their quarter-final qualification into doubt. Elsewhere, Sweden held off a resilient Bosnia and Herzegovina 3-2 in League B, while Premier League club action remains paused with Arsenal holding a five-point cushion atop the table.
