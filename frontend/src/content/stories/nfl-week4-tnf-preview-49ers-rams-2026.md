## Why this one matters

With Philadelphia's 24-20 Monday Night Football victory over Dallas officially in the books, the NFL calendar waste no time turning the page. Week 4 opens immediately on Thursday night with one of the fiercest rivalries in modern football: the Los Angeles Rams visiting the San Francisco 49ers at Levi's Stadium.

This represents the second meeting between the two franchises this season following their historic international matchup in Melbourne, where San Francisco rolled to a 27-7 victory behind three Brock Purdy touchdown passes. But returning to Northern California on just three days of rest creates an entirely different tactical landscape. Both Kyle Shanahan and Sean McVay understand that early divisional victories often determine home playoff seeding come January.

## Purdy versus Stafford under the lights

San Francisco enters the contest seeking consistency. Purdy continues to distribute the ball efficiently to George Kittle, Brandon Aiyuk, and Christian McCaffrey, but the 49ers defense must contend with a Rams aerial attack that remains explosive when Matthew Stafford finds protection.

For Los Angeles, Kyren Williams has shouldered the rushing load, but Stafford's connection with Puka Nacua is the engine that can disrupt San Francisco's zone coverage. Stafford was pulled late in the Melbourne defeat and will be motivated to avenge that performance in prime time before a hostile Santa Clara crowd.

## The numbers that tell the story

- **Matchup:** Los Angeles Rams at San Francisco 49ers.
- **Kickoff:** Thursday 1 October, 8:15 p.m. ET at Levi's Stadium.
- **Rivalry history:** San Francisco has won five of the last six regular-season home meetings against the Rams.
- **Purdy efficiency:** Purdy completed 72 percent of his passes in the Week 1 Melbourne meeting between these two clubs.
- **NFC West stakes:** The winner claims sole early advantage in one of football's tightest four-team divisions.
