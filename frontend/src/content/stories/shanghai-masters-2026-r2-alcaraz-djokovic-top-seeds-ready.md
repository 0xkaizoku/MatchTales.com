## Why this draw matters

The Rolex Shanghai Masters runs 7 to 18 October on hard courts. The posted prize fund is 9,415,725 dollars. Calling the tournament "round 2" skips a first round that is still being played. The top seeds, including Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz and Novak Djokovic, drew byes. They have not played a Shanghai match.

## What the week before was

On 6 October, Djokovic beat Alex de Minaur 7-6 (3), 0-1 retired in the China Open final. Alcaraz beat Jiri Lehecka 7-6 (4), 6-4 in the Japan Open final. Those are titles. They are not Shanghai matches. The round of 64 is the next time the byes play, and it is not already over.
