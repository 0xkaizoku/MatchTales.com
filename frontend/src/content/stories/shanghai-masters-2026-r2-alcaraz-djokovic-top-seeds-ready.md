## Why this match matters

The opening phase of the Rolex Shanghai Masters is complete at Qizhong Forest Sports City Arena, clearing the stage for the world's elite to enter the 96-player draw. With 1,000 ATP ranking points and year-end Finals qualification berths on the line, the tournament intensifies as the top 32 seeds shake off opening-round byes and begin their campaigns.

## What actually happened

Wednesday's first-round action winnowed the field, setting up marquee second-round matchups. Newly crowned Tokyo champion Carlos Alcaraz enters the tournament riding a formidable 10-match winning streak, having defeated Jiri Lehecka 7-6(4), 6-4 on Tuesday to defend his Japan Open title. The Spaniard anchors the bottom half of the draw, brimming with confidence on Asian hard courts.

On the other side of the draw, Novak Djokovic brings an immaculate record to Shanghai after lifting his seventh China Open crown on Tuesday when Alex de Minaur retired trailing 7-6(3), 0-1. Djokovic, a four-time champion in Shanghai, seeks his 103rd career ATP trophy and 41st Masters 1000 shield. With world No. 1 Jannik Sinner resting, Germany's Alexander Zverev holds the top seed position in the top half.

## What happens next

Second-round matches begin Thursday and Friday, testing whether the titleholders from Beijing and Tokyo can sustain their physical intensity across back-to-back Asian weeks. For early-round underdogs, catching a top seed in their tournament opener presents the ultimate upset window.
