## Primetime in Inglewood to wrap Week 5

Week 5 of the 2026 NFL season saved its grand finale for Southern California as the Buffalo Bills visit the Los Angeles Rams at SoFi Stadium on Monday Night Football. Both franchises enter the national spotlight needing a signature primetime statement after a chaotic Sunday that reshuffled divisional hierarchies across the league.

Sunday night set a breathtaking standard in Atlanta, where the Baltimore Ravens outlasted the Falcons 30-23 in overtime. Lamar Jackson connected with Mark Andrews on a fourth-quarter touchdown drive before Tyler Huntley scored on a goal-line quarterback sneak in extra time, capped by Kyle Hamilton's diving interception on Michael Penix Jr. to seal the victory. Meanwhile, the Minnesota Vikings and San Francisco 49ers both improved to 5-0 with gritty afternoon wins over the Saints and Seahawks respectively.

## Allen versus Stafford under the canopy

Tonight, Josh Allen leads Buffalo's high-powered passing attack against Matthew Stafford and Puka Nacua under the multimillion-dollar canopy of SoFi Stadium. With Sean McDermott's defense looking to contain Kyren Williams on the ground, Monday Night Football promises an electrifying conclusion to one of the wildest weeks of the young season.
