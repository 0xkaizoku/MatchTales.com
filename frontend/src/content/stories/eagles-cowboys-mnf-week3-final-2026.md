## Why this one matters

Week 3 of the 2026 NFL season concluded with one of the most intense fixtures in football, and Philadelphia held firm when the pressure peaked. The Eagles defeated the Dallas Cowboys 24-20 at a thunderous Lincoln Financial Field, securing a 2-1 record and sole possession of second place in the NFC East.

Nick Sirianni built this victory on physical football. Jalen Hurts directed the offense with poise, passing for 240 yards and a touchdown while adding his 45th career regular-season rushing score on a classic goal-line surge. Alongside him, Saquon Barkley provided the hammer, rushing for 116 yards on 22 carries and breaking loose for a decisive 18-yard touchdown run early in the fourth quarter.

## The defensive stand that settled it

Dallas refused to go quietly under the prime-time spotlight. Dak Prescott completed 24 of 38 passes for 262 yards, connecting with CeeDee Lamb for two touchdowns, including a 33-yard strike that pulled the Cowboys within four points with five minutes left on the clock.

With 1:12 remaining, Dallas faced fourth-and-6 at the Philadelphia 28-yard line. As Prescott dropped back to scan the end zone, veteran defensive end Brandon Graham and tackle Jalen Carter collapsed the pocket from the interior, forcing an incomplete pass on a hurried throw. The stop preserved the lead, triggered pandemonium in South Philadelphia, and officially closed the Week 3 slate.

## The numbers that tell the story

- **Final score:** Philadelphia 24, Dallas 20.
- **Ground dominance:** The Eagles outgained Dallas 164 to 72 on the ground, led by Barkley's 116 rushing yards.
- **Milestone:** Hurts recorded his 45th career rushing touchdown, extending his franchise quarterback record.
- **Standings impact:** Philadelphia improves to 2-1 behind Washington (3-0); Dallas drops to 1-2.
- **Looking ahead:** The NFL moves immediately toward Week 4, with Thursday Night Football featuring an NFC West showdown between the 49ers and Rams.
