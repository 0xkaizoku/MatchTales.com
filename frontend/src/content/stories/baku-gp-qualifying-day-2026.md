## Why this session matters

Formula 1 reaches the sharp end of its rare Thursday-Saturday timetable at the 2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix. With Sunday reserved for national Remembrance Day in Azerbaijan, the full 51-lap starting grid will be established Friday afternoon around the Baku City Circuit's 6.003-kilometer layout.

## What actually happened

Following Thursday's productive practice sessions along the Caspian shoreline, Formula1.com and Sky Sports outline Friday's progression from FP3 into one-hour knockout qualifying at 1:00 p.m. UK time (4:00 p.m. local Baku time).

Mercedes enters the session as the team to beat. Rookie sensation Kimi Antonelli commands the drivers' standings on 292 points after his landmark victory in Madrid, while George Russell holds second on 211 points. The Silver Arrows lead the constructors' championship with 503 points, ahead of Ferrari (358) and McLaren (306).

The technical demand in Baku is unforgiving. Teams must trim aerodynamic drag for the flat-out 2.2-kilometer straight while maintaining enough mechanical grip to navigate the tight 7.6-meter Turn 8 pinch at the Old City fortress. Lewis Hamilton, Charles Leclerc, and Lando Norris have tuned downforce levels specifically to contest Mercedes in Q3.

## What to watch next

Grid position in Baku is crucial. With qualifying concluding on Friday, drivers will have only 24 hours to finalize race strategies for Saturday's 51-lap Grand Prix starting at 12:00 p.m. UK time.
