## Highbury Stadium prepares for Tyneside invasion

The magic of English domestic knockout football produced its next romantic chapter on Thursday, 29 October, as the official Carabao Cup quarter-final draw pitted fourth-division Fleetwood Town against Premier League powerhouse Newcastle United. Highbury Stadium on the windswept Lancashire coast, which witnessed Fleetwood's unforgettable penalty shootout elimination of Arsenal on Tuesday, will host Eddie Howe's high-flying Magpies in mid-December.

For Fleetwood manager Charlie Adam and goalkeeper David Harrington, the hero of the shootout who denied Bukayo Saka, the draw represents the ultimate validation of their historic run. "To bring a club of Newcastle's stature and European pedigree to Highbury Stadium is everything cup football is about," Adam told reporters following the announcement. "Our supporters lived through a dream against Arsenal, and now they get another night under the lights against one of the most formidable teams in the country."

## Heavyweights avoid each other in last-eight pairings

The remainder of the quarter-final draw kept the tournament's top seeds apart, setting up fascinating tactical battles across the country. Manchester City, fresh from their hard-fought 2-1 victory over Brighton on Wednesday, will welcome Brentford to the Etihad Stadium as Pep Guardiola pursues his fifth League Cup triumph. Liverpool, who dispatched Chelsea 2-0 at Anfield behind Dominik Szoboszlai's 104 km/h stunner, were handed a tricky London assignment against Crystal Palace at Selhurst Park. In the remaining tie, Tottenham Hotspur host Southampton.

Meanwhile, attention now pivots to Saturday's colossal Premier League Matchweek 9 encounter between Arsenal and Liverpool at Emirates Stadium. Mikel Arteta's Gunners, level with Manchester City at the league summit on 21 points, are eager to banish their midweek cup blues against Arne Slot's title contenders. With cup euphoria still echoing across Lancashire, the stage is set for a pivotal autumn weekend across English football.
