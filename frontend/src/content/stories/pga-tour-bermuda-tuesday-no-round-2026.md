## Tuesday is empty

No round of the Butterfield Bermuda Championship is being played on Tuesday, 20 October. The first round is Thursday, 22 October, at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton. The PGA Tour lists the course at 6,828 yards, par 71, designed by Robert Trent Jones. The field is 120 players. The purse is $6 million, with $1,080,000 to the winner. Adam Schenk is the defending champion. Golf.com and the PGA Tour list Golf Channel coverage from Thursday through Sunday, 1 to 4 p.m. ET. A pro-am is on the Wednesday schedule. There is no Tuesday leader.

## What still stands

Michael Brennan's win at the Baycurrent Classic, at 16 under, is still the last PGA Tour result on this desk. The Associated Press, in its Tuesday golf note, lists Corey Conners as the highest-ranked player in the Bermuda field at No. 61 in the world, and names amateurs Miles Russell and Mason Howell. Schenk is trying to become the first player to defend this title, and the PGA Tour lists him at No. 163 in the FedExCup.
