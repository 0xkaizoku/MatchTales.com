## Why this one matters

Moving Day at the Sanderson Farms Championship produced fireworks in Mississippi as Keith Mitchell put on a ball-striking display to claim the solo 54-hole lead at Country Club of Jackson.

Starting the day four shots behind overnight leader Beau Hossler, Mitchell came out firing on Saturday morning. The Georgia product birdied five of his first seven holes, displaying razor-sharp iron play and converting putts from all distances. By the time he walked off the 18th green with a tap-in birdie, Mitchell had posted an 8-under 64, bringing his total to 20-under-par 199.

Hossler proved he would not go quietly. The 29-year-old, seeking his breakthrough PGA Tour victory after multiple runner-up finishes, carded a bogey-free 4-under 68. He will play alongside Mitchell in Sunday's final pairing just one stroke behind at 19-under.

## What is on the line on Sunday

For players competing in the FedExCup Fall series, every shot carries career-altering consequences:

- **Two-year exemptions:** A victory on Sunday secures full PGA Tour status through the end of 2028.
- **Major invitations:** The winner earns an automatic invitation to the 2027 Masters Tournament, PGA Championship, and The Sentry.
- **The chasing pack:** Defending champion Kevin Yu (67) and former US Open champion Lucas Glover (67) sit tied for third at 15-under, five shots back, ready to capitalize if the leaders falter.

With benign weather forecasted for Jackson on Sunday afternoon, golf fans can expect an aggressive shootout down the stretch as Mitchell and Hossler duel for the trophy.
