## Why this preview matters

Kimi Antonelli won the first Madrid Grand Prix. The championship is 292 to 211 to 191. The next race is not a Sunday in Europe. It is a Saturday in Baku.

## What we can print

Formula1.com: FP1 Thursday 24 September, FP2 the same afternoon, FP3 and qualifying Friday 25 September, Grand Prix Saturday 26 September. 51 laps of 6.003 km, 306.049 km. Sky Sports: qualifying 1 p.m. UK, race 12 p.m. UK. The official timetable notes Baku is four hours ahead of UTC.

Drivers after Madrid, unchanged: Antonelli 292, George Russell 211, Lewis Hamilton 191, Lando Norris 186, Charles Leclerc 167. Constructors: Mercedes 503, Ferrari 358, McLaren 306. Practice has not started.

## What we will not print

A Baku pole. A castle-section crash. A safety car. A Saturday winner. Come back after 26 September.
