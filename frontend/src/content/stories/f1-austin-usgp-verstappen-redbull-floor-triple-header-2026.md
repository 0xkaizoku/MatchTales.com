## Why this table matters

The next Grand Prix is Singapore, a sprint weekend from 9 to 11 October at Marina Bay. The race is listed for Sunday 11 October at 8:00 p.m. local time. It is not a finished race, and it is not the United States Grand Prix.

## What the official table says

Formula1.com's drivers' table, matched by AFP on 7 October as the standings ahead of Singapore: Kimi Antonelli 320, George Russell 236, Lewis Hamilton 214, Charles Leclerc 191, Lando Norris 188, Max Verstappen 188. That is an 84-point lead for Antonelli. Oscar Piastri is 7th on 128. Mercedes are on 556 from their two drivers. Ferrari are on 405 from Hamilton and Leclerc. McLaren are on 316 from Norris and Piastri.

## What the last race was

The last race on the board is the Bahrain Grand Prix held at Sepang, won by Max Verstappen on 4 October. George Russell won in Baku before that. A classification that already gives Norris a Singapore win does not match a table published ahead of Singapore. This desk does not print one, and it does not print an Austin grid penalty that is not on the official standings page.
