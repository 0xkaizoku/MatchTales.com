The Formula 1 paddock begins setup in Nevada on Monday, 16 November, with just three days remaining until cars hit the track for opening practice at the third running of the Las Vegas Grand Prix (19-21 November). While 20-year-old sensation Kimi Antonelli (390 points) and Mercedes (652 points) have already wrapped up both World Championships, the fight for second place in the Drivers' Championship represents one of the closest multi-driver shootouts in recent F1 history.

Just 33 points separate second-placed Max Verstappen (279 points) from sixth-placed Lando Norris (246 points) with three rounds remaining in Las Vegas, Qatar, and Abu Dhabi. Packed between them are Mercedes driver George Russell (262 points), Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton (261 points), and Charles Leclerc (249 points). The 6.201 km Las Vegas Strip Circuit, with its 1.9 km flat-out blast past iconic neon landmarks, will place a supreme premium on straight-line aerodynamic efficiency and tire warm-up in chilly desert night temperatures.

The Constructors' Championship runner-up battle is equally contested. Ferrari hold second on 510 points, but Red Bull Racing (415 points) and McLaren (398 points) remain within striking distance as teams prepare for Thursday night free practice. No competitive track sessions have taken place yet, but the tension across the Las Vegas boulevard is already at fever pitch.
