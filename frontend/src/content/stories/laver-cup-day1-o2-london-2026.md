## Why this tournament matters

Flushing Meadows is completed, and international tennis moves under the roof at The O2 in London for the 9th Laver Cup. The event marks the first tournament under new team captains: former Roland Garros champion Yannick Noah leads Team Europe, while eight-time major champion Andre Agassi commands Team World.

## What actually happened

Laver Cup organizers and Sky Sports confirmed the Friday Day 1 match sheet. The opening day session kicks off at 13:00 local time on the custom black court. Casper Ruud opens the tournament for Team Europe against Argentina's Francisco Cerundolo. Match 2 follows immediately, featuring Czech rising star Jakub Mensik against American Brandon Nakashima, who joined Team World after Ben Shelton's withdrawal.

The evening session brings a marquee singles contest followed by opening doubles. Noah's European squad includes Carlos Alcaraz, US Open champion Alexander Zverev, Flavio Cobolli, and Rafael Jodar. Agassi counters with Australian Alex de Minaur, US Open finalist Taylor Fritz, Learner Tien, and Alexander Bublik.

Under the Laver Cup scoring format, every victory on Friday earns one point. Saturday victories award two points, and Sunday matches are worth three points each. The first team to reach 13 points claims the cup.

## What to watch next

Day 1 sets the tone for the entire weekend in London. Can Agassi's squad seize early momentum away from home, or will Europe establish dominance before the point values double on Saturday?
