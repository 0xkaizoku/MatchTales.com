The NBA pauses across the league for Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, 26 November, giving all 30 rosters a breather after an explosive slate of Emirates NBA Cup group games on Tuesday and Wednesday nights.

Tuesday evening delivered fireworks in the nation capital, where the Boston Celtics poured in 80 first-half points (34-28 Q1, 46-21 Q2) to overwhelm the Washington Wizards 131-116. Jayson Tatum led the charge with 41 points, six rebounds, and eight assists, while Paul George caught fire from deep (6-of-9 threes) to finish with 28 points and nine assists alongside Neemias Queta 21 points. Washington fought back in the second half behind Anthony Davis (33 points, 13 rebounds), rookie AJ Dybantsa (20 points, 11 rebounds, seven assists), and Trae Young (17 points, 12 assists).

Elsewhere across Tuesday Emirates NBA Cup action, Cooper Flagg stuffed the stat sheet with 24 points, six rebounds, and 12 assists as the Dallas Mavericks dismantled Nikola Jokic (18 points, 11 rebounds) and the Denver Nuggets 120-101 on the road. In Orlando, shorthanded Detroit (playing without injured Cade Cunningham) rolled past the Magic 104-86 behind Isaiah Joe 21 points and Daniss Jenkins 15-point, 10-assist double-double before the league breaks on Thursday ahead of Friday full tournament return.
