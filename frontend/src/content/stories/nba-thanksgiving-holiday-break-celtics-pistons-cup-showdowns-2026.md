The NBA pauses across the league for Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, 26 November, giving all 30 rosters a breather after Emirates NBA Cup group games on Tuesday. Wednesday's nine-game slate is unpublished as final.

Tuesday evening delivered fireworks in the nation's capital, where the Boston Celtics poured in 80 first-half points (34-28 Q1, 46-21 Q2) to overwhelm the Washington Wizards 131-116. Jayson Tatum led the charge with 41 points, six rebounds, and eight assists, while Paul George finished with 28 points and nine assists. Washington fought back in the second half behind Anthony Davis (33 points, 13 rebounds) and Trae Young (12 assists).

Elsewhere across Tuesday Emirates NBA Cup action, Cooper Flagg stuffed the stat sheet with 24 points, six rebounds, and 12 assists as the Dallas Mavericks beat the Denver Nuggets 120-101 on the road, Jamal Murray scoring 20. In Orlando, Detroit (without injured Cade Cunningham) rolled past the Magic 104-86 behind Isaiah Joe's 21 points before the league breaks on Thursday.
