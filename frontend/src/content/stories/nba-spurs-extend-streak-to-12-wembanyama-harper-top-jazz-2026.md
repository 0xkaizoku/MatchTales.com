The San Antonio Spurs continued their incandescent start to the 2026-27 NBA campaign on Sunday night, 15 November, securing their 12th consecutive victory with a 110-102 road win over the Utah Jazz at Delta Center. The triumph pushes San Antonio's league-best record to 12-1, marking the franchise's longest regular-season winning streak in a decade.

Victor Wembanyama showcased his complete two-way arsenal in Salt Lake City, finishing with 26 points, 11 rebounds, 6 assists, and 5 blocked shots in 34 masterful minutes. The 7-foot-4 French center altered the entire geometry of the floor, denying Utah at the rim while connecting on 3 of 6 attempts from beyond the arc.

Down the stretch, it was 20-year-old rookie guard Dylan Harper who delivered the knockout blow. Harper poured in a career-best 31 points, including an electric 18-point explosion in the fourth quarter on 7 of 9 shooting. With Utah closing the deficit to two points midway through the final frame, Harper orchestrated a decisive 14-4 run with three consecutive driving layups and a step-back three-pointer. Mo Bamba led Utah with 26 points and 12 rebounds, but the Jazz had no answer for San Antonio's late-game execution. Across the league on Sunday, the Los Angeles Lakers overcame Phoenix 131-123 behind Anthony Davis, while the Boston Celtics routed Dallas 131-94.
