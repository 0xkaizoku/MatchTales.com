## Why this round matters

The BMW PGA Championship is the DP World Tour flagship. After 54 holes it had two names at 13 under and no winner. Sunday printed a winner. AP: JJ Spaun, 17 under.

## What actually happened

AP via WTOP: Spaun 70-66-68-67, 271. Manuel Elvira 69-66-68-70, Rory McIlroy 69-67-69-68, Aaron Rai 66-69-72-66 and Adam Scott 67-67-71-68, all 273. Filippo Celli, Matt Fitzpatrick, Ryan Fox and Joost Luiten 274. Ryan Gerard, the 36-hole leader, 67-65-75-71, 278.

Heart: Spaun a final-round 67 with six birdies after an early bogey. Elvira needed a birdie on 18 to win, found a fairway bunker, then chipped into a bunker and made a double bogey. FOX and ESPN print the same 17-under total.

## What we will not print

A playoff that did not happen. A 62. A Presidents Cup result. Medinah is still 24-27 September. Heart: the next DP World Tour event is the FedEx Open de France at Le Golf National.
