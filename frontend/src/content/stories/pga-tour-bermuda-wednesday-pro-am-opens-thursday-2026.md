## Pro-Am complete in Southampton

The Butterfield Bermuda Championship concluded its Wednesday Pro-Am at Port Royal Golf Course in Southampton, Bermuda. The official tournament begins Thursday, 22 October, running through Sunday, 25 October. The par-71 Robert Trent Jones layout measures 6,828 yards along the Atlantic coastline. The purse is $6 million, with the champion taking home $1.08 million and 500 FedExCup points. Golf Channel will broadcast live rounds Thursday through Sunday from 1:00 to 4:00 p.m. ET.

A full field of 120 players is scheduled for the opening round, with the 36-hole cut set at the top 65 players and ties. Defending champion Adam Schenk aims to become the first repeat winner in tournament history. Canadian Corey Conners enters as the highest-ranked participant at world No. 61. Past champions Brendon Todd, Seamus Power, Camilo Villegas and Rafael Campos are also in the field alongside amateurs Miles Russell and Mason Howell.

## Brennan's mark remains untouched

No competitive stroke has been played on the tournament scorecard on Wednesday. Michael Brennan's triumph at the Baycurrent Classic, completed at 16 under par with a closing 68, stands as the most recent official PGA Tour victory. With FedExCup Fall ranking points on the line, players outside the top 100 face essential stakes to secure full tour privileges for 2027.
