## Why this match matters

This weekend is the Bahrain Grand Prix held at Sepang in Malaysia, 2 to 4 October 2026. It is not Austin, and it is not a finished Singapore Grand Prix. Singapore is 9 to 11 October. The race is Sunday 4 October at 15:00 local, 07:00 UTC. ESPN still listed the race as the next session, with no classification, when this desk was filed. No winner is printed.

## What actually happened

Qualifying on Saturday 3 October: Max Verstappen, Red Bull, 1:35.130, his first pole of 2026 and the 49th of his career. Lewis Hamilton, Ferrari, 1:35.428, 0.298 seconds back. Isack Hadjar, Red Bull, 1:35.558, then a five-place grid penalty for a seventh engine, which drops him to eighth. The race front row is Verstappen and Hamilton. Kimi Antonelli was fourth in 1:35.631 and starts third. Charles Leclerc was fifth in 1:35.666 and starts fourth. Reuters and The Race print the same order. Verstappen led Q1, Q2 and Q3.

## What it changes

The championship entering this race, after Azerbaijan: Antonelli 302, Russell 236, Hamilton 199, Norris 186, Leclerc 179, Verstappen 163. Constructors: Mercedes 538, Ferrari 378, McLaren 306, Red Bull 263. The last completed race was Azerbaijan. George Russell won it. Sky Sports: Verstappen second, 0.196 seconds back, Hadjar third, 10.704 back. Antonelli lead is 66 points. The Sepang race is the next session, and it is not in the book.
