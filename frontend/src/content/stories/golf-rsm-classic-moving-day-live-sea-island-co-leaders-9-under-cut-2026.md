Saturday Moving Day is officially underway at Sea Island Golf Club on the Georgia coast, where Round 3 of The RSM Classic is sorting out both the tournament trophy and the final PGA Tour cards for 2027.

Following two demanding rounds across the Seaside and Plantation courses, three players share the 36-hole lead at 9-under-par 131: American Michael Thorbjornsen, Englishman Marco Penge, and Denmark Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen. All three posted consecutive rounds in the 60s, navigating breezy coastal winds to establish a one-shot cushion over the chasing pack.

The 36-hole cut line was established at 2-under-par 138, sending 65 players through to the weekend on the Seaside Course. Crucially for the FedExCup Fall finale, key bubble contenders made the weekend to keep their tour privileges alive, including Austin Smotherman (-4), Mackenzie Hughes (-4), and Joel Dahmen (-3). With just 36 holes remaining in the 2026 PGA Tour calendar, every birdie putt carries life-changing consequences.
