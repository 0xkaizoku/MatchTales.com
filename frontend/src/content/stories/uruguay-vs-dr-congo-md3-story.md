Dallas. The roof was closed at AT&T Stadium, but the roof might as well have blown off from the intensity of the clash. Uruguay defeated DR Congo 2-0 in a match that showed the gritty, uncompromising side of South American football.

Federico Valverde was the heart of the Uruguayan performance. The midfielder bossed the pitch, breaking up play and driving La Celeste forward. DR Congo, led by the solid Chancel Mbemba, defended bravely and tried to catch Uruguay on the counter through the dangerous Yoane Wissa.

The pressure eventually paid off. Darwin Nunez opened the scoring with a powerful header from a corner kick, sending the Uruguayan fans into raptures. Valverde then sealed the game in the second half with a sensational long-range strike. The leopards fought until the final whistle, but Uruguay's defense kept them at bay.
