## Game 2 is tonight, and it is not final

The Atlanta Dream visit the Golden State Valkyries for Game 2 of the WNBA Finals on Monday, 19 October. ESPN and the WNBA schedule list tip at 8:00 p.m. ET at Chase Center in San Francisco, on USA Network, Peacock and CNBC. The WNBA lists Golden State leading the series 1-0. This desk does not post a Game 2 score.

Game 3 is Wednesday, 21 October, at 8:00 p.m. ET at State Farm Arena in Atlanta.

## What Game 1 actually was

ESPN and CBS Sports both list Game 1 as Valkyries 68, Dream 64 on Saturday, 17 October, at Chase Center. CBS reported Gabby Williams scored 26 points and the Golden State bench scored 20. Angel Reese finished with 14 points and 12 rebounds and was scoreless in the second half. Allisha Gray, cleared to play with a hand injury, led Atlanta with 19 points.

That is the series: Golden State 1, Atlanta 0. Anything else on an older MatchTales card for 82-76 is withdrawn. The number on the page tonight is 68-64, and Game 2 is still to be played.
