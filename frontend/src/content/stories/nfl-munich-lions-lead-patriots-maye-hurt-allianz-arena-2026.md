The NFL's Week 10 international series touched down in Germany on Sunday, 15 November, where the Detroit Lions took command of the New England Patriots, building a 21-3 halftime lead at Allianz Arena in Munich. Broadcast live on FOX, Dan Campbell's Lions struck early and often through the air and on the ground in front of a raucous sellout crowd of over 70,000 fans.

Disaster struck the Patriots on their second offensive possession when starting quarterback Drake Maye was brought down on a third-down sack by Aidan Hutchinson, injuring his left ankle. Maye was helped to the locker room and quickly ruled out, forcing backup Tommy DeVito into action. New England's only first-half points came courtesy of Andy Borregales' 33-yard field goal.

Detroit capitalized ruthlessly with a 56-yard touchdown strike from Jared Goff to Amon-Ra St. Brown, a 12-yard touchdown pass to Isaac TeSlaa, and a bruising 5-yard touchdown run from Jahmyr Gibbs. The game remains actively in progress during the second half in Munich, with the main NFL Sunday slate kicking off across North America at 1:00 p.m. Eastern.
