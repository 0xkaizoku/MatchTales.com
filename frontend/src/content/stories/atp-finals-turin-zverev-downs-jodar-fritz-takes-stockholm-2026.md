The 2026 Nitto ATP Finals got underway Sunday, 15 November at the Inalpi Arena in Turin with world No. 1 Alexander Zverev defeating Spanish teenage sensation Rafael Jodar 6-4, 6-4 in their John McEnroe Group round-robin opener. Zverev served masterfully across 87 minutes, dropping just seven points on his first serve and securing a single break in each set to open his campaign for a third season-ending championship.

The evening session in Turin features American Ben Shelton taking on Frenchman Arthur Fils in the second McEnroe Group clash, marking the first French singles appearance at the ATP Finals in a decade. Action in the Ivan Lendl Group begins Monday with second seed Carlos Alcaraz meeting Frances Tiafoe and Daniil Medvedev facing Flavio Cobolli.

On Saturday, top seed Taylor Fritz secured his second Stockholm Open trophy at the Kungliga Tennishallen, edging Argentina's first-time finalist Thiago Agustin Tirante 6-2, 4-6, 7-6 (3). Fritz built an early lead before Tirante rallied with ferocious forehands, but the American steadied in the deciding tiebreak to seal the title before boarding his flight to Turin.
