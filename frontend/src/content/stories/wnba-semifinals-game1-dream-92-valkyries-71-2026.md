## Why this match matters

Atlanta Dream beat New York Liberty 92-82 on Sunday 4 October and lead the best-of-five semifinal 1-0. Golden State Valkyries beat Las Vegas Aces 71-60 the same afternoon at Chase Center and also lead 1-0. Game 2 is not a result on this desk. Regular-season records stay where StatMuse left them: Lynx 33-11, Valkyries 32-12, Aces 31-13, Dream 30-14.

## What actually happened

ESPN recap, game 401918295: quarters Liberty 24-16-15-27, Dream 21-16-32-23. The Liberty led by 3 at half. Atlanta won the third quarter 32-15. Jordin Canada scored 25, with 20 in the second half, and had 5 steals. DeWanna Bonner scored 23. The AJC had Angel Reese on 17 points and 7 rebounds, and Rhyne Howard on 16. Sabrina Ionescu scored 27 and Breanna Stewart 19. Allisha Gray left in the second quarter with a left-hand injury. Game 2 is Wednesday in Atlanta.
