## Zverev survives three-set war of attrition

The Rolex Shanghai Masters produced high drama over the weekend as the world's best tennis players navigated tricky conditions at Qizhong Forest Sports City Arena. Second seed Alexander Zverev had to dig into his deepest reserves on Sunday, overcoming French big-hitter Quentin Halys 6-4, 6-7(8), 6-3 in a two-and-a-half-hour Center Court battle that tested his baseline endurance to the limit.

Elsewhere in the draw, 2023 Shanghai champion Hubert Hurkacz demonstrated pristine hard-court form by seeing off wild card Yi Zhou 6-2, 3-6, 6-3 behind 19 aces. Meanwhile, Australia's Alex de Minaur extinguished the fairytale run of 19-year-old Japanese sensation Rei Sakamoto with a disciplined 7-6(6), 6-4 victory, while Frenchman Arthur Gea claimed the upset of the round by ousting Ben Shelton in two tiebreak sets.

## Sinner and Alcaraz step onto Center Court

Monday brings the final slate of round of 32 clashes and sets the stage for the round of 16 under the retractable magnolia roof. Top seed Jannik Sinner and world number two Carlos Alcaraz return to action aiming to assert their authority on the fast DecoTurf courts, with the Shanghai crown and 1000 ranking points hanging in the balance.
