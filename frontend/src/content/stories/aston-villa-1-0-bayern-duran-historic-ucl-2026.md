## Why this one matters

Forty-two years after Peter Withe etched his name into European football immortality in Rotterdam, Villa Park hosted an electric reprise that will live just as long in Midlands legend. Aston Villa defeated Bayern Munich 1-0 on Champions League Matchday 2, ending the German champions' 41-match unbeaten streak in the group and league phase.

Unai Emery crafted a masterclass of defensive discipline. Bayern arrived in Birmingham averaging four goals per match under Vincent Kompany, with Harry Kane and Jamal Musiala leading an explosive attack. Yet Villa resisted every wave with composure, anchored by goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez who delivered seven saves of staggering quality, including a diving tip-around-the-post from Kane deep in stoppage time.

The decisive breakthrough arrived in the 79th minute. Pau Torres curled a magnificent clearance into the path of Jhon Duran, who had entered the match nine minutes earlier. Glancing up from 30 yards out, the Colombian striker spotted Manuel Neuer lingering off his line. With supreme technical audacity, Duran struck a first-time left-footed lob that curled over the stranded German icon and settled into the net.

The roar from the Holte End shook the foundations of Trinity Road. Duran sprinted to the corner flag, mobbed by teammates as Prince William celebrated in the directors' box. With six points from their opening two European ties, Aston Villa have announced that their return to continental football is a campaign of genuine ambition.
