The final chapter of the 2026 PGA Tour season opened on Tuesday, 17 November, as competitors took to the Seaside and Plantation courses at Sea Island Golf Club for official practice rounds ahead of The RSM Classic (19-22 November).

Hosted by World Golf Hall of Fame member Davis Love III on St. Simons Island, Georgia, the tournament serves as the ultimate pressure cooker in professional golf. For the players ranked near the bubble in the FedExCup standings, every shot across the coastal links carries monumental consequences. Only the top 125 players on the points list after Sunday's final round will earn fully exempt status for the 2027 PGA Tour season, avoiding the uncertainty of conditional status and qualifying school.

Simultaneously, competitors ranked between 51 and 60 are battling for the Aon Next 10 exemptions, which grant lucrative entries into the early-season Signature Events including the AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am and The Genesis Invitational. Local favorite Davis Thompson arrives with strong momentum, joined in the field by two-time RSM Classic winner Mackenzie Hughes and Bermuda Championship victor Austin Smotherman.

The tension at Sea Island follows a celebratory weekend across the Atlantic, where Rory McIlroy captured his record-tying eighth DP World Tour Race to Dubai title after Tyrrell Hatton secured a five-shot victory at the DP World Tour Championship.
