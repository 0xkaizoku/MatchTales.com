England delivered a historic performance under the floodlights in Madrid on Sunday, 15 November, conquering European champions Spain 3-2 in front of 63,788 stunned spectators at Riyadh Air Metropolitano. The result ended Spain's extraordinary 43-match unbeaten run in competitive international matches dating back to March 2023, propelling Thomas Tuchel's squad above La Roja to secure top seeding in UEFA Nations League Group A3.

The Three Lions took control on the stroke of halftime. After a sweeping team move resulted in an English penalty, captain Harry Kane calmly tucked the ball into the lower left corner on 45 minutes, celebrating his 73rd international goal on his 126th cap. The breakthrough rattled the hosts, and England capitalized immediately following the intermission. In the 53rd minute, defender Ezri Konsa rose above the Spanish backline to head home a corner, before Ben White finished a sweeping counter-attack on 58 minutes to hand England an improbable 3-0 advantage.

Luis de la Fuente's European champions refused to capitulate quietly. Mikel Oyarzabal breathed life into the stadium with a 65th-minute penalty after a handball decision, and winger Nico Williams slashed the deficit to one with a crisp 75th-minute strike. England faced severe adversity when midfielder Elliot Anderson received a straight red card for an 84th-minute challenge, leaving the visitors down to ten men for seven tense minutes of added time. Jordan Pickford produced two acrobatic saves in stoppage time to preserve the historic victory, securing a seeded quarter-final berth for England in March 2027 while Spain advance as runners-up.
