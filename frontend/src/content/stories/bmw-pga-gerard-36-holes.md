## Why this round matters

The BMW PGA Championship is the DP World Tour flagship. After 36 holes at Wentworth it has a leader and it does not have a winner. That is the honest line on 18 September.

## What actually happened

DP World Tour leaderboard: Ryan Gerard 67-65, 132, 12 under, alone. Adam Scott 67-67 and Filippo Celli 68-66, both 10 under. Manuel Elvira 69-66 and Aaron Rai 66-69, both nine under. Five players at eight under, including Rory McIlroy 69-67 and Open champion Ryan Fox 68-68.

PA: McIlroy a five-under 67, four shots behind Gerard. Gerard eagled 17 from about 20 feet and birdied 18 for a bogey-free 65. AP: Jon Rahm 78-77, last of the 140 players who finished 36 holes, missed the cut. PA: Tommy Fleetwood missed the cut at three over.

## What we will not print

A champion. A Sunday 62. A collapse that has not happened. Rounds 3 and 4 are 19-20 September. The Presidents Cup is still 24-27 September at Medinah.
